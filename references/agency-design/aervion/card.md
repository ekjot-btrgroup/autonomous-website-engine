# Aervion  (agency-design)
A quiet, confident portfolio-studio homepage for one product designer. It wins on discipline: pure white and warm stone surfaces, one typeface pushed very tight and very large, generous 120px rhythm, and a single acid lime used only as tiny status dots. Personality comes from small technical details such as dot-matrix numerals and an availability pill.

## Type
- Inter throughout. H1 88px / 600 / -0.05em / 0.98 leading (64px tablet, 44px phone), stacked over three short lines, left aligned.
- H2 56px / 600 / -0.045em, always two lines; H3 28px and H4 20px at 500 for card titles.
- Body Large 20px / 1.5 in grey #6e6e73 for intros; Body 16px / 1.6; small 14px.
- Eyebrows: 12px / 500 / 0.08em uppercase in ink, led by a small square or round bullet.
- LED Dot dot-matrix face at 16px in light grey for 01 to 06 indexes: the one quirky typographic note.
- Footer display wordmark 318px / -0.06em / 0.85 leading, filling the full width.

## Colour
- White #ffffff page, stone #f4f4f1 for alternate sections and cards, #e9e9e4 and #e3e3de for stronger fills and hairlines.
- Ink #0e0e10 for headlines and the black pill buttons; grey #6e6e73 body, #a3a3a8 tertiary.
- Accent lime #d4ff3f appears only as 6px dots (availability, eyebrows) and a soft radial glow inside the near-black CTA card.
- No shadows at all; separation by fill, 1px hairlines and generous whitespace.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px canvas, 32px side padding, 120px vertical padding per section; white and stone sections alternate.
- Radius scale: 24px to 28px for large photo and CTA cards, 20px for service cards, 14px for small cards, full pills for buttons and chips.
- Section headers split: heading on the left two thirds, short grey intro aligned to the right column at the heading's baseline.
- Grids: 2 columns for projects with large square images, 3 columns for services, 4 columns of stone tiles for client names.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: white nav with round logo mark, wordmark, availability pill with green dot, plain text links and black pill CTA. Under it a three-column meta row (label over value) closed by a hairline. Then eyebrow, giant three-line headline, and a split row with grey intro paragraph on the left and two pill buttons plus a tiny note on the right. Finally a wide rounded portrait image with a frosted name chip in the bottom left, gently parallaxing.
2. Selected work: split header, then a 2 by 2 grid of big rounded project images, each with title on the left and category and year in small caps on the right, a one-line description below, and a centered outline pill to see all.
3. About preview on stone: left a rounded image with a dark label sticker, right an eyebrow, three-line H2, a long first-person paragraph and a secondary note, then a small pill button.
4. Services: split header, six stone cards in 3 columns, each with dot-matrix index top left, black dot top right, title and short grey description pinned to the bottom. Below, a white outlined strip with a one-line prompt and a black pill.
5. Clients: centered eyebrow and two-line heading with intro, then eight stone tiles with a client name and a tiny category label.
6. Testimonials on stone: split header, then four white quote cards in 2 columns with large quote text, a round dark avatar, name and role.
7. Closing CTA: near-black rounded banner with lime dot eyebrow, two-line white heading, grey line and a white pill button, warmed by a blurred olive-lime glow in the top right corner.
8. Footer: big underlined email address on the left, three small link columns, then the full-width wordmark and a thin legal row.

## Motion
- Calm: elements fade up 12px over 1s with an expo-out curve (0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) on load and on view.
- One parallax on the hero portrait at speed 90.
- No tickers, loops or scroll choreography; restraint is part of the brand.

## Phone
- Meta row stacks into label and value pairs; headline wraps to five or six short lines at 44px.
- All grids collapse to one column; service cards keep the index and dot corners.
- Footer wordmark scales to 94px and still spans edge to edge.

## Signature moments
- The meta strip plus availability pill: a studio that reads like a status board.
- Dot-matrix numerals against heavy Inter: technical warmth without colour.
- A single acid lime touch, used so sparingly it feels like a signal light.
- Edge-to-edge wordmark as the final beat of the page.

## Borrow
- Split section headers (heading left, intro right) for an editorial grid feel.
- Stone cards with index top left, status dot top right and text anchored to the bottom.
- Tiny coloured status dots in eyebrows and buttons instead of a full accent palette.
- Dark CTA card lit by one blurred colour glow.

## Avoid
- The page is very calm; for a louder brand add one stronger visual moment rather than more colour everywhere.
- Client names in plain tiles must be real or clearly marked placeholders.
- The meta strip duplicates the eyebrow content; keep only one of them if copy is thin.
- Long first-person bio paragraphs in the about block need real material from the brief.
