# Agnos  (agency-design)
A friendly, conversion-minded studio homepage. Everything is warm off-white and near-black, and one hot orange carries all the energy: a single word in the headline, primary buttons, rings, the popular plan. The craft is in the tactile surfaces, white cards seated inside soft grey trays like objects in a box, and in small human touches such as handwritten notes and emoji ratings.

## Type
- One family, Geist 500, tracking -0.03em everywhere for headings: h1 70px / 1.1 (56px tablet, 40px phone), h2 52px / 1.2 (40px, 36px), h4 36px, h5 22px for card titles.
- Body Geist 500 at about 16-18px in warm grey #69686e, line height 1.2 to 1.4; small text 14px at 400.
- Eyebrow labels: Geist 600, 12px, 0.15em tracking, uppercase, wrapped in small chevron brackets and sometimes a faint boxed chip.
- Caveat 18px in orange is used sparingly as a handwritten aside next to buttons and headings, often with a looping arrow.
- Accent emphasis comes from colour, never weight: the last word or phrase of a headline turns orange.

## Colour
- Page #f5f4f3 and #faf9f8, cards pure white, trays and dividers #e7e2dd and #eeecea.
- Ink #060612 for headings and the secondary solid button; muted grey #69686e for body.
- Accent orange #ff6321 with darker #cf4e17 for gradients; orange at 15% for soft fills behind rings and emoji.
- Big orange surfaces appear only twice or three times (bento CTA card, featured plan, final CTA), each as a warm gradient from light peach to full orange.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px canvas, content tray around 1000px, generous 160px top padding before each section, sections run back to back without bottom padding.
- Container recipe: a light grey tray with 1px border and 16px radius, holding white cards with 8px radius at a 20px gap. This nesting repeats for compare, bento, pricing and the CTA.
- Bento grid on 4 columns with mixed spans; pricing on 2 equal columns.
- Buttons are compact 8px radius rectangles with a subtle inner shadow, orange primary next to black secondary.
- Dashed hairlines separate list rows and frame the logo row.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: floating compact pill nav at top centre (logo, burger, black button). Centered eyebrow chip, two-line headline with orange last word, short grey sub, orange and black buttons, a script note with arrow beside them. Behind it, three pale embossed concentric arcs on each side carry square icon tiles that rotate very slowly. A dashed-rule logo row closes the hero.
2. Gallery: two image rows ticking in opposite directions under a centered white badge card with a big count and a button; edges fade into the page.
3. Compare: tray with two side-by-side lists, a plain "others" column against an orange-tinted "us" column with check rows, plus a small rating cluster.
4. About bento: CSAT card with a five-emoji scale, wide strategy card with photo and arrows, orange gradient booking card with fanned portrait cards, three progress rings, a case card, an industries list that fades at top and bottom, and a founder portrait on orange.
5. Services: left column sticky heading with button and script note, right column of stacked white service cards with icon tile, short copy and tag chips.
6. Projects: centered heading, wide slideshow card pairing project meta on the left with a large image on the right, peeking next slide, dot pager.
7. Process: tall sticky stage where a single step card swaps content as the visitor scrolls, with a dotted vertical rail and numbered marker.
8. Pricing: two plans in a tray, white starter and orange gradient featured plan, inner price panel, dashed divider, check list and faint watermark icon in the corner.
9. Testimonials: two cards per slide with long quote, avatar, name and star row, arrows below.
10. Blog: one tall featured post with a big image beside a stacked list of small posts.
11. FAQ: centered accordion of white rows with plus icons, then an avatar stack and a small help prompt.
12. CTA and footer: orange gradient tray with headline and button on the left and a booking mini card on the right; footer with newsletter field, link columns and contact block.

## Motion
- Load: sections fade up 20px with a 0.5s spring; headline text reveals per character with 10px blur.
- Ambient: hero icon arcs rotate over 150s loops; gallery and industry tickers drift at velocity 30 and slow on hover.
- Scroll: sticky services column and a scroll-target variant swap in the process section.
- Overall lively but controlled; nothing parallaxes or scales aggressively.

## Phone
- Nav stays a compact floating bar. Headline drops to 40px and wraps to four lines; buttons stack full width.
- Bento collapses to one column in the same order; rings stay in a row of three.
- Compare lists stack, pricing stacks with the featured plan second, project slides become stacked cards.

## Signature moments
- Orange last word in every big headline plus a script aside: friendly without losing polish.
- Embossed concentric arcs with drifting tool icons as a quiet, crafted backdrop instead of a photo.
- Tray-and-card nesting that makes every block feel like a physical object.
- Emoji satisfaction scale and progress rings as visual proof devices rather than plain numbers.

## Borrow
- The tray (grey, 1px, 16px) holding white cards (8px) as a reusable container system.
- One accent colour used on a single word per heading, plus one or two full gradient blocks per page.
- Chevron-bracketed uppercase eyebrows and dashed hairline dividers for a technical-crafted feel.
- Opposing double ticker under a centered badge card for a work wall.
- Sticky left heading with scrolling service cards on the right.

## Avoid
- The page is long and dense; trim to the strongest bento tiles rather than copying all of them.
- Fake-looking proof (ratings, percentages, client counts) must come from real brief facts or be marked as placeholders.
- Caveat notes lose charm if used more than two or three times.
- The floating "get it" badge at bottom right is template chrome, not part of the design.
