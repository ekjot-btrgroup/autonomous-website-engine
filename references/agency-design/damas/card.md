# Damas  (agency-design)
A branding studio homepage that runs on two surfaces: a deep bottle green page and pale, grain-textured panels that sit inset inside it. Heavy condensed headlines, one sunny yellow, and motion-blurred photography give it a confident, slightly streetwise energy without getting loud.

## Type
- Display Anton 400: H1 105px/1.0 at -0.02em in all caps (84 tablet, 62 phone); H2 80px/1.1 in title case (50 phone); H3 46; card titles about 38.
- A secondary Satoshi 500 heading scale (48, 42, 36) exists for calmer blocks; body Satoshi 500 at 16 to 18px/1.4, -0.02em.
- A small script (Damion 18px) appears once as a client signature above a quote; it is the only flourish.
- Small uppercase yellow kicker labels sit above every H2.

## Colour
- Green #063d30 is both the page colour and the ink colour on light panels, so the brand colour carries text and ground.
- Light panels #fafafa with a fine noise grain; cream #faf7f3 as an alternate.
- Yellow #fcd718 for pill buttons, kicker labels, the brush stroke and the active pricing CTA; nothing else is yellow.
- Hairlines are white at 10 to 20 percent on green; images get a thin pale outline.

## Layout and spacing
- Light sections are rounded panels inset 20px from the page edge, so the green shows as a frame around them; green sections run full width with 120px vertical padding.
- Section heads alternate: centred head with kicker, or left-aligned H2 with a small "start a project" arrow link at the far right.
- Cards: 20 to 25px radius, image fills the card, title in Anton over a dark gradient at the top, one line of copy at the bottom.
- Pill nav floats at the top centre: a green capsule holding a yellow logo dot and white pill links.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero on a pale panel: H1 caps two lines left, one-line intro, yellow pill CTA; right, two overlapping tilted photo cards with a yellow brush lightning mark bridging them.
2. About on green: H2 left with paragraph, a wide photo right with small sticker-like yellow tags.
3. Services on a pale inset panel: H2 left, link right, three tall image cards with titles over the photo.
4. Selected works on green: two large alternating split cases (text one side, big image plus a vertical strip of two mini thumbs on the other), then three ruled list rows, then a yellow pill.
5. Testimonials on a pale panel: script signature, large quote, link; tilted portrait photo right with a yellow brush mark.
6. Pricing on green: centred H2 over two lines, monthly or yearly switch, three white plan cards with check lists, middle CTA yellow.
7. Journal on a pale panel: three image cards with Anton titles below.
8. FAQ on green: huge H2 left, dark accordion bars right, small contact pill below.
9. CTA and footer on a pale panel: caps headline left with arrow box, stacked tilted media cards right; footer contact columns and socials.

## Motion
- Light touch: 14 appear effects (fade from y 10px, spring 1.6s bounce 0, 0.8s delay on the hero) and 11 text effects (word by word, 10px blur, spring 1.8s).
- Layout template adds smooth scroll, a fixed blur gradient at the viewport edge, a noise overlay and click effects; the floating nav stays fixed.

## Phone
Single column with green margins around each pale panel; H1 drops to 62px caps over the image cards; service cards stack full width; project rows keep name, date and discipline in a tight stack; pricing cards stack.

## Signature moments
- Tilted, overlapping photo cards with a hand-painted yellow mark.
- The green page reading as a frame around pale inset panels.
- Featured case splits that taper into a compact ruled project index.

## Borrow
- Brand colour as both ground and ink for a strong, two-tone identity.
- Inset panel rhythm to alternate light and dark without hard full-width bands.
- Hybrid portfolio: two hero cases, then an index list with thumbnails.
- A single hand-drawn accent shape repeated in two or three places.

## Avoid
- Pricing on a studio homepage unless the client really sells fixed packages.
- Repeating the same placeholder case description on every project.
- Too many all-caps Anton lines; keep caps to the H1 and use title case elsewhere.
