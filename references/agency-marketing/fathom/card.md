# Fathom  (agency-marketing)
A growth and performance agency site that sells data-driven services with the calm of a literary journal. Warm parchment bands, a tight condensed serif and dreamlike classical imagery make a numbers business feel crafted and premium rather than salesy.

## Type
- Display: Libre Caslon Condensed, regular/medium, tracking -0.04em throughout. Scale 75 (h1, 56 tablet, 48 phone), 56/1.05 for service rows, 48/1.0 stats, 36-40 section titles, 20-28 card titles. Single words switch to the italic cut for emphasis in the CTA headline.
- Body/UI: Geist 500 at 13-18px, also tracked -0.04em, leading 150% for paragraphs, 110-120% for labels. Uppercase 13px with +0.08em only for small caps labels on dark.
- Hierarchy comes from the serif/sans contrast and huge size jumps (56px service names beside 13px tag chips), never from bold weights.

## Colour
- Cream #ede8dc and Cream Deep #e3dccb alternate section by section to create bands with no borders.
- Ink #1c1a18 for headlines, pill buttons and the one full dark pricing band; Ink Soft #5a554c for body.
- Terracotta #b5654a appears only as the 6px eyebrow dot (and tiny accents); Gold #a98b5d sparingly.
- Tag chips are a slightly darker cream fill with ink text; hairlines are warm grey-beige.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 canvas, 64px side padding, 80px top/bottom per section, content full width between paddings.
- Every section opens with the same header kit: dot + label left, "Context 2026" right, hairline, then title left and a "/N/ ©" counter right, another hairline.
- Radii: 20-24px on image cards, 6px on chips, full pills on buttons and circular "+" toggles. No shadows, no borders beyond hairlines.
- Image cards carry a dark gradient at the bottom with a serif title and one-line sans caption on top.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-viewport fixed image (parallax 110, scales to 0.95 as you scroll) behind a 800px spacer so the cream page slides up over it like a curtain; preloader with centered wordmark first, content fades in after 2.3s.
2. Logos: 6 small rounded client cards in one row on Cream Deep.
3. About: very tall (about 2000px) band with a sticky scroll-reveal statement and four ivy sprig PNGs drifting on scroll.
4. Services: accordion rows, giant 56px serif name left, cream tag chips right, circular "+" button, hairline between rows; short paragraph and black pill CTA beneath.
5. Why choose us: split, tall rounded image card left (with logo and caption), right a 36px serif statement then 3 ruled stat rows (big number, serif label, two-line sans note).
6. Selected work: 2x2 grid of image cards with numbered corners, then a stat strip of three 48px figures in a rounded cream panel.
7. How we work: three numbered process columns (01/02/03) separated by rules, CTA row below.
8. Testimonials: portrait photo left, large serif quote right, round prev/next buttons.
9. Pricing: full ink band, three rows (plan name, feature lines, price with small CTA) with a monthly/yearly pill toggle.
10. Team: 4 cards with small portrait, name, role; CTA row.
11. Journal: one featured article card plus compact list cards.
12. FAQ: plain serif question rows divided by hairlines.
13. Contact: big rounded image card with serif headline (italic last word) and two icon-led promises, underline-only form and contact chips right; footer ends with an oversized cropped wordmark.

## Motion
- Appear on 50 nodes: fade-up 20px, spring 1.2s bounce 0.1, staggered; hero waits 2.3s for the preloader.
- Scroll style transforms: hero scales 1 to 0.95 (spring stiffness 800, damping 300), leaves drift; parallax speed 110 on hero media.
- Lenis smooth scroll, progressive blur components on image edges, sticky scroll-reveal text in About.

## Phone
Single column with 20-24px padding, service rows stack name over chips, stats become stacked rows, cards full width. Display drops 75 to 48 and 56 to 34; nav is a fixed compact bar with menu.

## Signature moments
- The cream page sliding over a fixed hero image.
- Ivy cut-outs floating across an empty parchment band while a statement reveals word by word.
- Giant condensed serif service names beside tiny chips.
- Oversized cropped wordmark at the foot of the page.

## Borrow
- The repeatable section header kit (dot eyebrow, year label, rules, counter) for instant editorial rhythm.
- Two-tone same-hue banding instead of borders or cards.
- Accordion service rows with tag chips as a scannable alternative to service cards.
- Underline-only form fields beside a rich image card for a calm contact block.

## Avoid
- The 2000px near-empty About band loses people without the live animation; shorten it in static output.
- Surreal AI imagery is a strong taste call; swap for client photography when the brand is literal.
- Very low-contrast small Ink Soft text on Cream Deep at 13px.
