# Impacta  (agency-marketing)
A social media and creator-marketing agency site that feels like a polished SaaS page. Light grey canvas, white cards and a single raspberry red make it clean, while portrait video tiles and red line icons keep it social and lively.

## Type
- Inter only. H1 64px/1.1 weight 600 at -0.04em (48 tablet, 40 phone); H2 48px/1.2; H3 36; H4 24; H5 18.
- Body Inter 500 at 12-18px with -0.02 to -0.03em, grey #575757.
- The device: in each H1 and H2 one phrase turns raspberry ("Win On Social", "Services", "Results"), so colour does the emphasis instead of weight or italics.

## Colour
- Page #eeeeee, cards #f9f9f9 to #fdfdfd, hairline strokes #cdcdcd.
- Accent #ff003d for headline phrases, the nav CTA pill (with a soft red glow shadow), icon strokes and pricing highlights; 10-20% tints for badges.
- Near-black #1c1c1c for text and secondary pill buttons; dark sections are rare.

## Layout and spacing
- Centred section heads: small grey pill tag, two-line H2 with accent phrase, two-line grey intro.
- 100px vertical padding throughout; content about 1080 wide.
- Cards: white frame with 6px inner padding holding a 20px-radius grey inner panel (image or icon) beside copy, giving a double-border look.
- Pills: full radius buttons, black primary with icon, white secondary with hairline.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: centred H1 with red second line, subcopy, black and white pill buttons, avatar stack with star rating; below, a horizontal ticker of tall portrait video cards with handle chips, then a logo strip.
2. Showcase: floating social platform icons around a centred statement, then metric rows (label left, huge figure right).
3. Results: three big counting figures with labels and dividers, CTA.
4. Services: three wide cards alternating icon panel left or right with title, two-line copy, check list and black CTA; they stick and dim as you scroll.
5. Mission: video tile with two animated counters.
6. Projects: 2-column grid of campaign image cards with tags.
7. Process: three sticky step cards with icons.
8. Comparison: two-column "others vs us" checklist card with the brand column highlighted.
9. Pricing: three plan cards with toggle, middle plan tinted pink, plus a wide custom-plan bar.
10. Testimonials: large featured quote plus two tickers of small review cards.
11. FAQ: H2 left with accent line, accordion right.
12. Blogs: two large image cards.
13. CTA in footer: big phone mockup with H2 and form.

## Motion
- Appear: y 100px fade, spring stiffness 300 damping 80, 0.8s delay in hero; 68 appear nodes overall.
- Text effect on H1: character by character with 10px blur, spring 0.7s bounce 0.
- Tickers at velocity 50 (hover slows to 50%); scroll-variant counters; style transform on stacked service cards (opacity 0.5, y -32px, scale 0.96); sticky process and FAQ heads; Lottie line icons.

## Phone
Single column with 20px padding; H1 40px; portrait ticker continues; service cards stack image above text; pricing cards stack; comparison stacks.

## Signature moments
- Red phrase inside every headline.
- Creator portrait ticker right under the fold.
- Stacked service cards receding as you scroll.

## Borrow
- One-accent headline phrase system for instant brand recognition.
- Inset image panels inside white cards for a neat double-frame.
- Card stack recede effect for multi-service pages.

## Avoid
- Very long homepage (13 sections); trim for clients.
- Placeholder zero metrics in the static capture look broken.
- Generic SaaS section order; reorder around the client's strongest proof.
