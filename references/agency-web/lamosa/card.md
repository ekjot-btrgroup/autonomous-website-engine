# Lamosa  (agency-web)
A polished studio homepage aimed at SaaS founders. The page is a calm cool grey canvas where every block is a set of white cards seated inside a slightly darker grey tray, so the whole site reads like a tidy product dashboard. Contrast comes from black: black pill buttons, black icon chips and one inverted black card per tray. A coral red is used only as a pinpoint marker.

## Type
- Display: Satoshi 700, -0.05em tracking, 1.2 leading. H1 48px (42px tablet, 36px phone), H2 40px, H3 32px, H4 28px; H5 24px and H6 20px drop to 500 for card titles.
- Headlines are short and almost always split into two centered lines.
- Body: Inter 500 at 18px / 1.3 in grey; small 14px and 12px for card copy, list items and captions.
- Big numbers in stat and pricing cards use Satoshi at about 40px, paired with a 24px label underneath.

## Colour
- Page #ebecef cool grey, trays #d4d8dd, cards near-white #fcfcfd and #f6f7f8.
- Ink #0a0a0a for headings, buttons and icon chips; grey #616161 body; #a3a3a3 on dark cards.
- Accent coral #ff4d36 (and #e1443a) only for the small section-marker square, the "New" nav badge, the status dot and quote marks; a soft red pill marks "limited" items.
- Inverted cards use near-black #010309 with white text, giving each tray one strong dark anchor.
- Shadows are soft and grey under icon chips and floating cards; borders are rare.

## Layout and spacing
- 1136px content column framed by faint dashed vertical guide lines that run the full page height.
- Radius scale: 32px trays, 24px cards, 16px inner panels, 99px pills.
- Tray recipe: grey tray with 16px inner padding, white cards at 24px gaps, one card inverted or used as an inline CTA.
- Section header recipe: coral square, centered two-line H2, optional one-line grey intro.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: floating white pill nav on the right (dropdown, "New" badge, black pill CTA with arrow knob), logo on the left. Status pill, centered two-line headline, grey sub, black arrow pill plus white pill button. Two side columns of small quote cards tilt and fade in and out of focus. Below, a small logo ticker with a count line.
2. Features: wide slideshow card showing a project mockup in a tilted phone with labels.
3. Stats tray: four white stat cards (black icon chip, big number, label, grey line) plus a "let's start" CTA card and an awards list card.
4. Services: sticky left heading with button, right column of expandable service rows.
5. Process tray: four step cards in a 2 by 2 grid, each with a small UI illustration (video call, dashboard, launch chart, analytics line), then an inline CTA card.
6. Projects: 2-column grid of dark product renders on black, each with name, tag chips and a view link; centered outline button.
7. Pricing tray: three plans (light, highlighted white, inverted black with a "limited" pill), each with icon chip, price, full-width pill button and icon list; an add-on toggle row; then a wide quote card with a big result number beside a black pitch card.
8. Testimonials tray: six cards with a big result number, short quote and author, plus an inline CTA card.
9. FAQ: left heading and help card, right accordion of white rows.
10. Contact: two-column form with text inputs, phone field with country picker, checkboxes and a black submit pill; contact details on the left.
11. Blog and footer: two article cards, then a dark band with logo ticker and a centered two-line heading, and a light footer with newsletter field and link columns.

## Motion
- Spring fade-up of 24px (stiffness 66, damping 20) on most blocks, with long staggered delays in the hero.
- Hero quote card columns drift with parallax at speed 60 and fade at the edges.
- Smooth scroll and a fixed blur gradient at the bottom of the viewport; sticky services heading.
- Moderate overall: movement is ambient, never theatrical.

## Phone
- Nav collapses to logo plus menu button; headline 36px over two or three lines; buttons stack.
- Trays become single columns of cards with the same radii; the inverted card stays inverted.
- Very long page on phone because every card stacks; the hero quote columns are hidden.

## Signature moments
- Fading, tilted testimonial cards orbiting the hero headline as social proof.
- The grey tray with one black card: a repeatable system that makes sections feel related.
- Pill buttons with an inner white arrow knob.
- Tiny coral square as the only colour cue for each section.

## Borrow
- Tray and card nesting with a single inverted card per group for rhythm.
- Coral (or any brand colour) used only as section markers and badges on a neutral page.
- Big number plus label stat cards with a black icon chip that casts a soft shadow.
- Dashed vertical guides to give a quiet blueprint feel.

## Avoid
- The stats, awards and testimonial results are template filler: never reuse numbers without real brief facts.
- Too many trays in a row make the page monotonous; break with one full-bleed image or dark band.
- The floating buy badge and blur gradient are template chrome.
