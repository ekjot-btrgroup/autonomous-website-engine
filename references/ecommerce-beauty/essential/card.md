# Essential  (ecommerce-beauty)
A small natural-skincare store that keeps the UI almost invisible: white page, pale grey card frames, black pill buttons, and a warm book-serif for every heading. The botanical product photography does all the colour work, so the shop reads clean, gentle and trustworthy.

## Type
- Display: Erode 500, h1 56px / 1.2 (50px phone), h2 44px (36px phone), h3 32px, h4 26px, no negative tracking, title case.
- Section headings are two lines centered: line one in ink, line two in grey #575757 at the same size.
- Body: DM Sans 500 at 22, 20, 18 and 16px, leading 1.4; secondary copy in grey; everything at medium weight for a soft, even texture.
- Small pill tags (14px, grey fill) sit above every heading as section labels.

## Colour
- White #ffffff page, #f8f8f8 card frames and tags, #ebebeb hairlines.
- Ink #171717 for headings and solid pill buttons; outline pill for secondary actions.
- Orange-red #ff462e only on rating stars.
- Photography supplies sage, sand, cream and the occasional bright blue.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px content with 40px gutters, 100px top and bottom padding per section, 52px between header and content.
- Cards: 14-16px radius, pale grey frame with a small centered label above an inset image, no shadows, no borders.
- Centered hero and stat sections alternate with left-aligned split sections (heading left, list or cards right).

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: avatar stack with stars and a customer count, centered two-tone serif headline, short line, black pill button; a single tall product bottle crops off the bottom with a parallax drift.
2. About: small tag left, a long serif statement right in quotation marks.
3. Store: split header with outline view-all pill; asymmetric product grid of three small then two wide photo cards with name, maker and price under each.
4. Stats: centered serif stat headline with quote and button, framed by an arch of 20 or so circular product thumbnails.
5. Features: centered header; three sticky rows each with a tag chip, serif title and copy left and two framed image cards with labels right.
6. Blog: split header; one large featured post card, then three smaller image cards with read time.
7. FAQ: centered header and an accordion of rounded grey rows with chevrons.
8. CTA: wide rounded photo panel with a centered two-tone serif headline, rating and pill button; footer with a huge outlined serif wordmark bleeding off the bottom.

## Motion
- Hero elements appear on mount: y -20px (drop in from above), spring 400 / 80, delayed 1.2s after the headline.
- Headings: word-by-word blur reveal, y 10px, blur 10px, spring 200 / 80, 0.05s per word.
- Hero bottle parallax speed 70; thumbnail arch rotates 30deg into place on view (spring 200 / 60).
- Sticky feature rows, fixed nav with blur gradient, smooth scroll.

## Phone
- Hero stays centered with the bottle below; headline 50px.
- Product grid becomes one column; the thumbnail arch shrinks behind the stat.
- Feature rows stack: text first, then the two image cards side by side.

## Signature moments
- The arch of circular product thumbnails around a stat.
- Single hero product cropped by the fold.
- Two-tone serif headlines with a pill tag above.

## Borrow
- Pale grey framed cards with a small centered label above the image for a gallery-like shop.
- Ring or arch of round thumbnails to dramatise a single claim.
- Medium-weight sans body for a soft, approachable read under serif headings.
- Sticky split feature rows for value propositions.

## Avoid
- Centered two-tone heading plus tag repeated on every section becomes monotonous.
- Very similar card frames everywhere; vary scale or crop to keep rhythm.
- Thin stat claims without real data.
