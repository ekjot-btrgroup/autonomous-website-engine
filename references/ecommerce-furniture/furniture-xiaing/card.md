# Furniture XIAING  (ecommerce-furniture)
A slow-made furniture shop that sells mood before product: candle-lit rooms, dusk terraces, cream paper backgrounds and a single rust accent. The voice is a quiet grotesk, interrupted once per headline by a serif italic phrase, which makes the brand feel crafted and personal rather than catalogue-like.

## Type
- Inter Display throughout: hero h1 around 64px / 500 / -0.03em; section h2 64px with 74px leading (52px tablet, 40px phone); h3 26-36px.
- Instrument Serif italic at matching size for one emphasised phrase in the hero and the scroll-reveal paragraph; serif 12px uppercase +0.08em for small labels.
- Body 18-20px / 1.4 regular in warm grey #6a635e; meta 11-13px medium, uppercase caps labels with +0.14-0.16em tracking.
- Prices in 24px bold, product names 20px medium, which gives shop cards a confident retail rhythm.

## Colour
- Cream #f0ebe5 page and sand #e8e2da panels; espresso ink #1e1712 for text, pill buttons and the dark product band.
- Rust #a14d08 only for tag chips, category link labels and small caps eyebrows.
- White at 64-85% for text on photos; glass product cards on photos use translucent warm grey with backdrop blur.
- Photography carries the warmth: amber lamps, timber, olive greens.

## Layout and spacing
- 1440px canvas, 32px gutters, 120px vertical padding, 64px header-to-content gap.
- Section headers split: small label with hairline and big heading left, short paragraph and pill CTA right-aligned.
- Radius: 40px pills, 16px image cards, 12px glass panels; hero image inset 8px in a rounded frame; most structural blocks square.
- Thin black marquee bands with uppercase values and star separators frame the category and product sections.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed dim interior in a rounded inset frame, glass pill nav floating at the top, headline with serif italic phrase bottom left, short lead, circular arrow button; a small glass product card bottom right.
2. Scroll text reveal: large centered paragraph that brightens word by word from 10% grey as you scroll.
3. Categories: marquee band, split header, 4-column grid of tall photo cards offset in a stair step, each with a notched cream label tab carrying a rust link.
4. Craft: three sticky full-screen outdoor scenes that stack on scroll; each has a numbered category label, rust tag, hairline, two-line headline and a glass product card with add-to-cart and view pills.
5. Collection: espresso band with marquees top and bottom, 3x2 product grid of photo cards with badge, name, price, rating, small swatches and a two-line note.
6. Stats: split header and four large counters with captions, the last one in rust.
7. Journal: four article cards with image, category, title and read-more.
8. Newsletter and footer: centered serif-led heading with an inline email pill on a dark band, footer with link columns.

## Motion
- Appear on scroll target: y 60px, spring stiffness 280 / damping 50, replays.
- Style transform on scroll: product cards and headings drift y -72px with spring 400 / 60 as scenes stack.
- Text effect on hero: element by element, y 20px with 10px blur, spring 200 / 50, 0.6s delay.
- Scroll-reveal paragraph, three marquees at velocity 100, sticky craft panels, navbar changes variant on scroll, smooth scroll.

## Phone
- Hero keeps the inset frame with headline over the photo; product card drops under the copy.
- Category cards go one per row, keeping the notched label tab.
- Sticky scenes stay full-bleed but shorter; product grid becomes single column.

## Signature moments
- The serif italic phrase inside a sans headline.
- Sticky stacked room scenes with floating shoppable glass cards.
- Notched label tabs cut into category photo corners.

## Borrow
- Mood-led commerce: let full-bleed lifestyle scenes carry the product with a small glass card for price and actions.
- One-phrase serif italic swap for warmth without a second display family.
- Split section header (label and big heading left, copy and CTA right) for retail rhythm.
- Thin marquee bands with brand values as section dividers.

## Avoid
- The scroll-reveal paragraph is nearly invisible at rest; keep enough contrast.
- Overlong page with three sticky scenes; one or two is enough for most brands.
- Very many text styles (over 50) in the source; consolidate to a tight scale when reusing.
