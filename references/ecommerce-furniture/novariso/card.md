# Novariso  (ecommerce-furniture)
A furniture and interiors studio site for a brand that sells calm, natural-material pieces. The idea is restraint through scale: one grotesk family set enormous and tight, a paper background, sunlit beige photography and a single copper accent, with near-black bands giving the scroll a slow, gallery-like rhythm.

## Type
- Display: Inter Display 500, 112px, tracking -0.055em, leading 0.92 (84px tablet, 56px phone). Lines stack like a poster; the final line of the hero is coloured copper.
- H2: Inter Display 500, 88px, -0.05em, leading 0.95 (64 / 44). Used for every section title, always two lines, left aligned.
- H3 30px and H4 20px, -0.03em / -0.02em, for card titles and process steps.
- Body Large 18px / 1.5 for section intros; Body 15px / 1.55 in muted brown-grey.
- Label: Inter 500, 12px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em, copper on light, used as a kicker above each H2.
- Hierarchy comes almost entirely from size jumps (112 to 18) and weight staying at 500; no bold anywhere.

## Colour
- Paper #F4F0E9 is the page; oat #E9E2D6 marks product and collaboration bands.
- Ink #141311 is both the text colour and the fill of every dark band (intro, process, studio, recognition, newsletter).
- Copper #B5784A only on kickers, step numerals, the last hero line and the dot inside the primary button.
- Hairlines at 12% ink on light and 14% paper on dark separate list rows and process columns.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 frame, 32px side padding, 128px top and bottom on every section (hero 48 / 104). Very consistent rhythm.
- Section header pattern: kicker plus two-line H2 on the left, short paragraph plus pill button on the right, then a full-width grid below.
- Images have square corners; only buttons and small badges are round (100px pills). No shadows, almost no borders.
- Card grids: 3-up category tiles with caption under image, 4-up product row, 4-up hairline process columns, 3-up testimonial cards, 2-up tall collection cards.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: three-line display headline left (last line copper), paragraph, two pill buttons, then a hairline and a small contact row; square interior photo right with a floating pill badge (thumbnail plus "new" label) overlapping its bottom edge.
2. Statement band (dark): one H2 split across the full width, first half flush left and second half flush right, with a wide photo and short paragraph below.
3. Categories: 3x2 grid of tall interior photos with a small title and one-line caption under each.
4. Featured products (oat band): 4 product photos with name and price on one line beneath.
5. Process (dark): H2 left, pill button right, full-bleed-in-container workshop photo with parallax, then 4 hairline-topped columns with copper 01 to 04 numerals.
6. Materials: 4 tall texture swatches (wood, oak, stone, linen) with small captions.
7. Testimonials: 3 oat cards with quote, avatar and name pinned to the bottom.
8. Studio (dark): large H2 left, landscape interior photo right with parallax.
9. Collections: two tall image cards with text and pill button overlaid at the bottom over a dark gradient.
10. Trade / collaboration (oat): photo left, H2, paragraph and a checklist of hairline-separated bullet rows right.
11. Recognition (dark): full-width list rows with year left, title centre, source right, divided by hairlines.
12. Journal: 3 story cards, image over title and date.
13. Newsletter (dark): centred H2, email field with inline pill submit.
Footer: link columns on paper, then a giant edge-to-edge lowercase wordmark across the bottom.

## Motion
- Appear on nearly every block (45): fade from opacity 0, y 16px, tween 0.9s, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], threshold 15%.
- Hero headline uses a word-by-word text effect: opacity 0, y 48px, blur 8px, spring 1.1s with no bounce, 0.1s delay.
- Parallax speed 0.9 on the two big dark-band photos only. Sticky navigation. No tickers or loops.

## Phone
Everything collapses to one column at 32px sides. Display drops to 56px and H2 to 44px, keeping the tight tracking so headlines still feel poster-like. Card grids become single tall images, process steps stack with their hairlines, nav becomes a logo plus menu button.

## Signature moments
- Copper last line of the hero headline.
- The split statement headline pinned to both page edges in a black band.
- Dot-in-circle pill buttons, dark and light variants.
- Giant lowercase wordmark filling the footer width.

## Borrow
- Kicker plus two-line H2 on the left, paragraph plus button on the right as a reusable section header.
- Alternating paper and near-black bands at a fixed 128px rhythm to pace a long homepage.
- Hairline-topped numbered columns for process steps.
- Floating pill badge overlapping the corner of a hero image.
- One accent colour reserved for numerals, kickers and a single headline line.

## Avoid
- The collections band leaves the right third empty with only two cards; fill the row or centre it.
- Thirteen sections all built from the same header pattern can feel repetitive; vary at least two layouts.
- AI-staged interior photos are interchangeable; a real brief needs real product photography.
