# CUREVANTA  (health-medical)
A family clinic or multi-specialty practice site that earns trust through order and restraint. The UI is strictly black, white and a warm mist grey, letting colourful clinical photography carry the warmth, while consistent pill buttons, numbered cards and dark inset bands give it a calm, premium-corporate rhythm.

## Type
- H1: Inter Display 500, 68px, tracking -0.035em, leading 1.04 (54 / 42). Three short lines.
- H2: Inter Display 500, 46px, tracking -0.03em, leading 1.1 (38 / 32); card titles 22 to 32px.
- Stat: Inter Display 500, 64px, tracking -0.04em, leading 1.0.
- Quotes: Inter Display 400, 21 to 26px, leading 1.35 to 1.4, ink colour (not grey).
- Body Large 17px, Body 15px, Small 13px in Muted #6b6b66, leading 1.6.
- Eyebrow: Inter 500, 11px, caps, tracking 0.08em, inside a light grey pill with a dot.
- One weight (500) for all headings; hierarchy via size and ink vs grey.

## Colour
- Paper #ffffff page; Mist #f3f3f0 for cards and soft panels.
- Ink #111111 for text, dark bands, rating bar, footer and the round button discs.
- Muted #6b6b66 for body; on dark, white at 62%.
- No brand hue at all: black is the accent. Hairlines at 10% ink, 12% white on dark.

## Layout and spacing
- 1104px content within 48px side padding; standard sections 112px top and bottom.
- Big moments (hero, values, care approach, stats, CTA) are full-width dark or photo cards inset 12px from the edges with 28px radius.
- Section header pattern repeats: eyebrow pill and H2 left, grey intro paragraph and outline pill button right.
- Cards: Mist fill, 22 to 28px radius, inner photo inset 8px with its own radius, small white number chip on the photo corner, footer row with label and round plus button.
- Buttons: white or outline pill, label plus a 36px circular disc with a plus sign.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: inset photo card full height, floating dark translucent nav bar inside it, eyebrow, three-line headline, grey copy, two pill buttons; translucent glass stats panel bottom right with three ruled rows.
2. Trust: heading left, short copy and check list right, then a wide rounded photo with a small floating white caption card.
3. About: photo and text split, then 3 mist feature cards with icons.
4. Treatments: header row, 3-column grid of numbered photo cards with title, clipped description and Explore row.
5. Values (dark inset card): header plus 6 feature cells separated by hairlines.
6. Patient stories: 3 mist quote cards with stars and initial avatars, then a black bar with giant rating, stars and a pill button.
7. Why choose: photo plus 4 step cards in a grid.
8. Care approach (dark inset card): 4 feature cards.
9. Doctors: 4 portrait cards with name and role.
10. Stats (dark inset card): 4 big numbers with labels.
11. Insights: 3 article cards with photo, tag and date.
12. FAQ: header left with button, 7 accordion rows right.
13. CTA: inset photo card with centred heading and two pills.
Footer: dark, newsletter row, link columns, then a huge outlined wordmark across the bottom.

## Motion
- Hero image settles from scale 1.12 to 1 over 1.8s on load, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1].
- 68 appear effects: headers and cards fade and rise in sequence as they enter.
- Parallax speed 90 on the trust photo and CTA background.
- Fixed nav. No tickers or text splitting: confident but not showy.

## Phone
- Inset cards keep 12px gutters; all grids become single columns of full-width cards.
- Hero stats panel drops below the copy; headline 42px.
- Nav collapses into the same dark bar with a menu button.

## Signature moments
- Glass stats panel over the hero photo.
- Plus-disc pill buttons used everywhere.
- Black rating bar with an oversized score.

## Borrow
- A strictly colourless UI so photography provides all the colour.
- Repeating section header (eyebrow plus H2 left, intro plus button right) for instant consistency.
- Number chips on card photos to suggest order without a timeline.
- Dark inset cards every third section to break a long white page.

## Avoid
- 13 sections and four nearly identical feature-card blocks feel repetitive; merge them.
- Generic stock medical photos; replace with real staff and rooms.
- Stats and ratings are placeholder claims; only use real figures.
