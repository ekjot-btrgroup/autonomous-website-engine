# Therapist  (health-therapy)
A one-person counselling practice site that sells reassurance rather than expertise. Everything is slowed down: warm paper background, regular-weight serif headlines, soft interiors inside tall arches and a single deep pine green reserved for the main action. Nothing shouts, so the visitor reads it as a safe room.

## Type
- Display: Source Serif 4 400, 68px, tracking -0.01em, leading 1.06 (54px tablet, 40px phone). Set in three short lines, left aligned.
- Heading h2: Source Serif 4 400, 42px, leading 1.12 (36 / 30). Title h3: 500, 24px, tracking -0.04em.
- Body: Inter 17px, leading 1.65; Lead 20px in Ink Soft; Small 14px.
- Label: Inter 600, 12px, caps, tracking 0.08em, used as eyebrow and above prices.
- Hierarchy comes from size and colour (Ink vs Ink Soft), never from bold weights. Serif numerals (1, 2, 3, prices) are set at heading size to act as graphics.

## Colour
- Paper #f5f1ea is the page; Paper Deep #ece5da makes alternating bands (process, CTA, footer).
- Ink #22302a (green-black) for headings and body; Ink Soft #56615b for leads and captions.
- Pine #335a4d only on the primary pill button and nothing else; secondary button is a 1px Ink outline pill.
- Line #d8cfc2 hairlines separate steps, price rows and FAQ rows. No shadows anywhere.

## Layout and spacing
- 1120px content inside 40px side padding; sections 96px top and bottom (hero 72px top).
- Hero is a 50/50 split; fees, about and FAQ use a narrow left heading column and a wider right content column.
- Images: tall portrait ratio with a full-radius top (arch) and small bottom radius. Same mask on hero, three topic cards and the portrait, which ties the page together.
- Pills at 999px radius for buttons; everything else square. Hairline rules instead of cards.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: caps eyebrow, three-line serif headline, grey lead, filled pine pill plus outline pill, a hairline then a row of three dot-bulleted facts; tall arched room photo right.
2. Topics: heading left with an underlined text link right, then three equal arched photo cards with serif title and two-line grey description.
3. Process (deep paper band): heading plus lead, three columns each topped by a hairline, a big serif numeral, title and short body; underlined link below.
4. Fees: heading, lead and fine print left; right column is two ruled rows, each with caps label, large serif price and a small duration line.
5. About: arched portrait left, serif greeting heading, two body paragraphs, a small credentials line and an underlined link.
6. FAQ: heading left, right column of ruled accordion rows with serif question and a thin plus icon.
7. CTA band (deep paper): centred serif line, one grey sentence, single pine pill.
Footer: brand and blurb left, two small link columns right, a rounded outlined notice box, then a hairline and copyright row.

## Motion
- Section contents fade up 12px on scroll into view: tween 0.6s, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], threshold 15%, no replay.
- Topic cards scale to 1.02 on hover with a spring (0.4s, bounce 0.2).
- Sticky navigation. No tickers, parallax or text effects: motion stays at a whisper.

## Phone
- Hero stacks text first with full-width stacked pills, arched image below.
- Topic cards collapse into compact rows: small arched thumbnail left, title and text right.
- Process and prices become single columns with the same hairlines; nav is wordmark plus a Menu label with a hamburger.
- Display drops to 40px, headings to 30px.

## Signature moments
- The arch mask repeated across every photo.
- Big regular-weight serif numerals and prices sitting on hairlines.
- One green button colour against an all-paper page.

## Borrow
- A single shape motif (arch) applied to every image for instant cohesion.
- Ruled rows instead of cards for prices, steps and FAQs: calmer and more editorial.
- Narrow heading column plus wide content column for mid-page sections.
- Fact row with tiny dot bullets under the hero buttons.

## Avoid
- AI-staged interiors look generic if reused; swap for real, varied photography.
- Every section has the same 96px rhythm and left heading pattern, so a long page can feel monotonous; add one contrasting moment.
- Phone topic rows lose the arch impact; keep at least one large arched image on small screens.
