# Lustre  (health-wellness)
A boutique hair and beauty studio that wants to feel like a calm, expensive appointment. Ivory paper, cocoa and champagne accents, and a tall condensed serif that switches to italic for its second line give the page a fashion-magazine voice, while glassy captions over warm salon photos keep it modern.

## Type
- Instrument Serif 400 for all display: h1 76px / 1.04 / -0.02em (58px tablet, 42px phone); h2 48px / 1.08 (40, 32); h3 30px; quote 34px; stats 44px.
- Every major heading pairs an upright ink line with an italic line in cocoa #6b4f32.
- Body Inter: large 18px / 1.6 in 66% ink, body 16px, small 14px; buttons and nav 15px / 500.
- Labels: Inter 12px / 500 uppercase with +0.18em tracking in cocoa, led by a short hairline.
- Logo: serif uppercase with +0.08em tracking.

## Colour
- Background ivory #f7f4ef, white cards, sand #e8e1d6 for muted panels; hairlines at 10% ink.
- Cocoa #6b4f32 for italic heading lines, labels and the primary solid pill button; champagne #b99762 for small highlights.
- Glass whites (50%, 65% border) for caption panels over photos.
- Footer flips to cocoa with a huge faint serif wordmark.
- Soft radial glows in the hero and contact backgrounds.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px canvas, cards nearly edge to edge with roughly 24px gutters; consistent 60px section padding, so sections sit close together.
- Signature shape: rectangles with 24px radius on three corners and a much larger radius on one top corner, used on the hero image, about image and service cards.
- Circle crops (100% radius) for inset portraits and avatars; 14px radius on small buttons and inputs.
- Few shadows; depth comes from soft floating glass cards with light drop shadows.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: centered label, two-line serif headline (italic second line), short lead, cocoa pill plus outline pill; below, a wide arch-cornered salon photo with a tilted inset photo card at left and a tilted glass review chip at right.
2. About: tall arch-cornered photo with a circular inset photo overlapping its corner; label, two-tone heading, paragraph and outline button on the right.
3. Services: split header with copy and button right; 3x2 grid of tall photo cards, each with a frosted glass caption panel holding a serif name and two lines of copy.
4. Why us: heading, copy and a product photo left; 2x2 white cards right with a round icon, number, serif title and copy.
5. Testimonials: giant faint serif words in the background, one tilted white quote card with stars, serif quote and author row in front.
6. Gallery: centered two-tone heading, slow ticker of mixed-shape photos (arch, rounded, circle).
7. FAQ: heading and a sand help card with a cocoa button left, accordion of white rows right.
8. Contact: two-tone heading and contact details left, white form card right with a full-width cocoa submit pill; cocoa footer with link columns and huge faint wordmark.

## Motion
- Almost static: only the gallery ticker (velocity 40, no hover slow-down).
- Sticky nav pill. The tilted cards and glows carry the sense of motion visually.

## Phone
- Hero stays centered; the arch image and floating chips scale down below the buttons.
- Service cards stack one per row keeping the arch corner and glass caption.
- Headings drop to 42px and 32px but keep the italic split.

## Signature moments
- Upright plus italic two-line serif headings in two colours.
- One exaggerated rounded corner on every photo card.
- Frosted glass caption panels over salon photography.

## Borrow
- Asymmetric arch corner as a brand shape repeated across photos.
- Glass caption panel inside service photo cards to keep text readable on busy images.
- Tilted floating chips (review count, inset photo) to add life to a static hero.
- Big faint background word behind a single testimonial.

## Avoid
- Almost no motion; add gentle reveals when reusing.
- 60px section padding makes some sections feel crowded; give more air on content-heavy pages.
- Section geometry in this file reads offset (the hero is reported at y 600), so section crops in this folder are shifted; judge from the full screenshot.
