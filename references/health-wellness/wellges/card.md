# WELLGES  (health-wellness)
A premium spa and holistic studio site built from big rounded inset cards floating on a bone background. Its voice comes from a two-font trick: every heading is a tight modern grotesk that ends in a soft italic serif phrase, so it feels both clean and tender. Dark moss panels alternate with white and pale sage cards to give a slow, luxurious rhythm.

## Type
- Display: Inter Display 500, 82px, tracking -0.045em, leading 0.95 (72 tablet, 52 phone). Very tight, two lines.
- H2: Inter Display 500, 56px, tracking -0.04em, leading 1.02; H3 28px; H4 20px.
- Accent: Instrument Serif italic, set at the same size as the heading it finishes, coloured sage on dark and moss on light.
- Body Large 18px and Body 15px in Muted #7e837a, leading 1.55 to 1.6.
- Label: Inter 500, 12px, caps, tracking 0.08em, set inside small pill eyebrows with a dot.
- Hierarchy: weight stays 500 throughout; contrast comes from size, the serif switch and grey body.

## Colour
- Background #f3f1ec bone; Surface #ffffff for light cards.
- Deep Moss #1b201a for the big dark panels (services, featured price, footer); Ink #141712 text.
- Sage #cfddb9 as the soft accent: marquee band, highlighted card, italic words on dark, hover tints.
- Moss #4a5a3f for italic serif words on light. Line at 10% ink for row dividers.
- Buttons: white pill on dark/photo, dark pill on light, each with a tiny dot before the label.

## Layout and spacing
- 1120px content; full-width elements are inset 12px from the viewport with 24 to 32px radius, so the page reads as stacked cards.
- Sections use 120 to 160px top padding and no bottom padding, which keeps the vertical flow airy.
- Highlights: 4 equal tall cards (photo, dark, white, sage) with 24px radius and small numeric indexes.
- Pricing: 3 cards, the middle one dark. Testimonials: 3 cards, middle dark, right sage.
- Radius family 16 / 24 / 28 / 32 px, pills at 100px, round icon buttons. Very few shadows; separation via fill changes.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed photo inside a rounded inset card, eyebrow pill, two-line display with second line in sage, short grey copy, white plus glass pill buttons, a small floating info chip top right.
2. Marquee band: sage rounded strip scrolling a sentence that alternates grotesk and italic serif.
3. Intro statement: large centred-left paragraph in heading size with the last phrase in moss italic, small link button right.
4. Highlights: heading with italic second line, then 4 contrasting cards (photo, dark with italic word, white with floating sphere, sage with inset photo).
5. Services panel (dark inset card): heading with italic tail, side copy and button, then numbered ruled rows with title, short description and round plus icon.
6. Benefits: rounded photo left with a small glass caption chip, right column of three icon rows with titles and grey text.
7. Pricing: centred heading, 3 price cards with tag pill, huge number, booking pill; middle dark.
8. Testimonials: heading left, note right, 3 quote cards in white, dark and sage with avatar row.
9. Approach: 4 step cards with UI-chip illustrations on top and text below.
10. Gallery: heading plus a horizontal ticker of rounded photos.
11. Journal: 3 cards with photo, category pill, title and meta.
12. FAQ: sticky heading column left with button, accordion list right.
13. CTA: full-bleed landscape photo card with centred display and pill.
Footer: dark card with newsletter field, link columns, contact line and a giant edge-to-edge wordmark.

## Motion
- Hero image scales from 1.14 to 1 over 2.2s on load, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1].
- Display text reveals word by word: 24px rise, 8px blur, 1s each, 0.05s stagger, 0.2s delay.
- Section content fades and rises on view (50 appear effects in total).
- Buttons and cards hover with a spring (0.4s, bounce 0.2) and a white 24% tint.
- Marquee and gallery tickers at velocity 50, slowing on hover. Floating sphere loops scale to 1.25 over 4s, mirror.
- Parallax speed 90 on the benefits photo and CTA background; sticky FAQ heading.

## Phone
- Inset cards become full-width stacks with 12px gutters; highlight and pricing cards stack one per row.
- Display drops to 52px, H2 to 36px; service rows keep number, title and plus icon, hiding descriptions under the title.
- Nav collapses to wordmark plus menu pill; footer wordmark still spans the width.

## Signature moments
- Grotesk plus italic serif split in every heading.
- Contrasting card quartet (photo, dark, white, sage).
- Giant footer wordmark.
- Word-by-word blur reveal on the hero headline.

## Borrow
- Inset rounded full-width panels with a thin page gutter for big moments.
- Alternate card fills inside one row instead of identical cards.
- Numbered ruled service rows inside a dark panel.
- Tiny dot inside pill buttons and eyebrows as a consistent micro motif.

## Avoid
- Page is long (13 sections); trim for smaller brands.
- The serif accent on every single heading can become a tic; keep it for 3 or 4 key lines.
- Stock spa photography and crystals read generic; art-direct real images.
