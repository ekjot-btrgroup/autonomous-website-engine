# wellnessé  (health-wellness)
A boutique preventive-health or longevity clinic site that feels like a sand-coloured magazine. A single grotesk set in tight all caps, film grain over everything and blurred, desaturated photography make it hushed and expensive; khaki square cards and big pale numerals add structure without breaking the calm.

## Type
- Main heading: Plus Jakarta Sans 500, 74px, caps, tracking -0.03em, leading 1.1 (50 / 32). White on photo in hero and CTA.
- Section title: same family 500, 48px caps (40 / 26), centred, two short lines.
- Eyebrow: 14px caps 500, tracking 0.01em, centred above each title.
- Description: 14px 500, capitalised words, grey at 66%, leading 1.3, centred under titles.
- Blog headings 27px; testimonial quote set in a book serif around 20px for contrast.
- Hierarchy: only weight 500; scale and caps carry everything.

## Colour
- BG #eeece0 warm sand everywhere; BG-Dark #dddac9 khaki for cards and tiles.
- T1 #3d361e olive-brown ink for headings and buttons; T2 grey-brown at 66% for body.
- White only on photos and the small menu pill. No saturated accent: the palette is one hue in tints.

## Layout and spacing
- 1440px canvas, content held to a roughly 990px centred column; headings centred.
- Cards are square-cornered or 4px radius khaki blocks (radius 0 used 40 times), with a few 16px radius exceptions and 100px pills.
- Process section is a 3x2 grid mixing tiles with giant outlined numerals and photo tiles.
- Light glass (background blur) panels over photos for service tiles and the hero floating card.
- A fixed menu pill top right opens a side navigation overlay instead of a top nav bar.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: left half a heavily blurred frosted photo carrying the caps headline and short copy at bottom left; right half the sharp photo; small floating card bottom right.
2. Signs: centred caps title, three khaki cards each with a caps label, a soft ethereal image and a caps caption.
3. About: tall soft-focus flower image left, small portrait and doctor intro right, big white caps line over the image.
4. Process: centred title, then a 3x2 grid of numbered khaki tiles and photo tiles.
5. Services: full-bleed portrait background, four glass tiles with icon and copy, and an enormous wordmark across the bottom edge.
6. Pricing: two khaki price cards with pill tag, soft image, price and check list; a small fallback block below.
7. Journal: three image cards with text over the photo.
8. Reviews: portrait card with a stat chip plus a large khaki quote panel with arrows, and a tabbed row of reviewer names.
9. Why us: bento grid with a progress chart card, photo cards, a trust card and a big percentage.
10. FAQ: left title and contact chip, right accordion.
11. CTA and footer: full-bleed photo with caps headline, then a sand footer with logo, columns and links.

## Motion
- Mostly on-load fades: opacity 0 to 1 with a spring (0.4s, bounce 0.2).
- Hover crossfades at 0.5s ease [0.44, 0, 0.56, 1] on article cards.
- Smooth scroll component, fixed noise grain and fixed side navigation; no tickers or parallax.

## Phone
- Hero stacks: photo with blurred lower band, caps headline 32px.
- Card grids become single tall khaki cards; process tiles stack in pairs.
- Menu pill stays fixed top right.

## Signature moments
- Film grain plus blur giving a soft analogue haze.
- Giant SERVICES wordmark bleeding off the bottom of a portrait.
- Huge pale numerals as tiles in the process grid.

## Borrow
- One-hue tonal palette (sand, khaki, olive ink) for a quiet premium feel.
- Half-blurred hero panel to hold type over photography.
- Square khaki cards with centred caps labels for a clinical but soft look.
- Glass tiles over a full-bleed portrait for a services block.

## Avoid
- Low contrast grey-on-sand body copy can fail accessibility; darken T2 for real use.
- Capitalising every word in descriptions reads awkward; use sentence case.
- Blurry stock imagery everywhere loses meaning; keep one or two sharp hero images.
- Section structure sits inside one wrapper, so it is harder to reuse section by section.
