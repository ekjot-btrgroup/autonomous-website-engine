# Elowenra  (hospitality-hotels)
A boutique countryside retreat or small luxury hotel site that sells slowness. Everything is hushed: a stone-coloured page, a deep forest green used as big inset panels, a tall condensed serif that feels like a printed brochure, and generous 128px breathing room between ideas, so the page scrolls like a calm weekend.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif 400 throughout. H1 84px / 1.0 / -0.02em; H2 56px / 1.05; H3 36px; H4 26px for card titles; quotes 34px italic / 1.25.
- Body: Inter 400, 15px regular and 18px large, 1.6 leading, grey-green #5c655f.
- Eyebrow: Inter 500, 12px, uppercase, +0.14em, always led by a tiny leaf glyph.
- Buttons and nav: Inter 500, 14px, with a small sage dot after the label.
- Hierarchy is built from scale contrast only (serif headings never bold); headings often break onto two short lines with a full stop.

## Colour
- Page stone #e9e6dc; lighter surface #f4f2eb for testimonial cards and floating text cards.
- Forest green #16231f for text and for the two big dark panels and the footer; on dark, text is #f2f0e8 with a 64% muted variant.
- Sage #a8c38c only as tiny accents: button dots, card index numbers, the giant footer wordmark.
- Borders are 14% green on light and 16% cream on dark, used only on dark card outlines and ghost buttons.

## Layout and spacing
- Panels (hero, dark category block, location, CTA) sit 12px from the viewport edges with 32px radius, so they read as cards on stone rather than full bleeds.
- Content sections use 40px side padding and 128px top and bottom.
- Typical split: heading block left (about 45%), paragraph or image cluster right.
- Image masks are the signature: arch (260px top radius), circle, and a 24px card with one 320px swept corner.
- No shadows, no blur; all depth from colour blocks and masks.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: inset rounded full-bleed photograph with dark scrim, centred eyebrow, two-line serif H1, short paragraph, a cream filled pill and a dark ghost pill, then a tiny dot-separated line of offerings.
2. Introduction: H2 left, paragraph and text link right with a small founded and location note, then a row of four images in different masks (rounded rectangles, circle, arch).
3. Category panel: big dark green inset panel, H2 and intro left, then a 3 by 2 grid of outlined dark cards with photo, index number, hairline, uppercase label, serif title, two lines and an underlined link.
4. Gallery teaser: copy left; right a tall arch photo beside a circle photo stacked over a rounded square.
5. Testimonials: centred H2, three light cards with an opening quote mark, italic serif quote and small name and city.
6. Location: dark panel with an arch-topped village photo left, address row with pin icon and button right.
7. Slow stay: one wide landscape photo with a swept lower-left corner and a floating stone card holding H3 and paragraph.
8. Journal: header row with button right, three post cards with tag, date, title and link.
9. Closing CTA: inset rounded photo panel with centred two-line serif and one pill button.
10. Footer: dark green with logo, short text and button, three link columns, then a huge sage serif wordmark across the bottom.

## Motion
- Only 20 effects, all the same calm fade-up: opacity 0 and y 40px, 1.1s tween, ease [0.22,1,0.36,1], 0.15s delay, on mount and in view.
- No tickers, no parallax, no hover choreography; stillness is part of the concept.
- Sticky navigation bar.

## Phone
Single column; inset panels keep their 12px gutter and radius. H1 wraps to four short lines. The 3 by 2 category grid stacks into six full cards. Image clusters stack with masks preserved. Footer wordmark scales to full width.

## Signature moments
- Inset rounded panels that make the page feel like a stack of cards.
- Mixed arch, circle and swept-corner masks.
- Sage dot after every call to action.
- The oversized sage wordmark closing the page.

## Borrow
- 12px inset panels with large radius to give a hotel site an object-like, printed feel.
- Combining 3 mask shapes in one cluster for an editorial gallery teaser.
- Numbered category cards with index, hairline and uppercase label above a serif title.
- One slow, long fade-up (1.1s, soft ease) used consistently instead of many effects.

## Avoid
- Body copy is set with capitalise-every-word, which reads awkwardly; use sentence case.
- Two dark panels plus dark footer plus dark CTA can feel heavy; keep at most two.
- Very low contrast sage on stone if used for text.
- 128px padding everywhere makes the page long; tighten where content is thin.
