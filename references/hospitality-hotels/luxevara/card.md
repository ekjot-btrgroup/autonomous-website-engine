# Luxevara  (hospitality-hotels)
A city boutique hotel group site that swaps the usual serif romance for confident Swiss modernism. Huge tightly tracked grotesk headlines, a cool mist-blue and ink palette and analogue film photography give it a fashion-magazine edge, while one electric lime accent marks every action.

## Type
- One family: Inter Display. Display 132px / 0.9 / -0.055em (92, 58); Heading L 76px / 0.98 / -0.05em (58, 42); Heading M 34px; Heading S 21px.
- Body 15px / 1.6 and Body Large 19px / 1.45, both slightly negative tracked.
- Labels 12px, 500, uppercase, +0.06em, often led by a small lime dot.
- Signature device: headlines break onto two lines and the second line is pushed right by a lime horizontal rule, so the rule reads as an em dash drawn in colour.
- Big index numerals (01 to 04) in outline or tinted grey at heading size.

## Colour
- White paper and stone #f2f3f1 for light sections; mist blue #a9b7c4 for hotel and CTA bands; ink #0b0b0c for the experiences band and service cards.
- Lime #d2f64b only on the primary button, the headline rules, label dots and the footer wordmark.
- Muted grey #6e737a for secondary copy; hairlines at 12% ink.
- The contrast of cool blue, black and acid lime is the identity; photography is kept cool and grainy to match.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px canvas with only 32px side gutters, so headlines and wordmarks nearly touch the edges.
- 128px section padding (140px for the closing CTA).
- Section header pattern: big two-line heading left, short paragraph and pill button right, aligned to the heading baseline.
- Radii: pills at 100px, cards at 18 to 28px; one glass blur on the nav pill.
- Hotel cards: wide white cards with a photo on the left half, number, name, tagline, small amenity text and a black pill.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed grainy street photo; glass pill nav centred with a lime pill CTA right; small uppercase meta line (districts, year, address) across the top; giant white lowercase wordmark across the full width; bottom left label and two-line headline with lime rule; bottom right a glass info chip, paragraph and two pill buttons.
2. Hotels (mist): heading left, big outline number and paragraph right; three sticky stacked hotel cards with index numbers that pile up as you scroll.
3. Services (white): split header, then four black square cards with huge grey numerals, short title and text.
4. Brand statement: three-line headline with lime rule, paragraph and button right, then a fact row of five label and value pairs separated by hairlines.
5. Experiences (ink): heading with label right, a six-row numbered table (index, title, description, category tag), then a paragraph and lime button bottom left and a giant phrase with lime rule bottom right.
6. Planned around you (stone): portrait photo left, label, two-line heading, paragraph and black pill right.
7. Gallery: split header then an auto-scrolling strip of tall photo cards with small captions.
8. Booking CTA (mist): centred giant two-line headline with lime rule, paragraph and black pill.
9. Footer: black with large phone and email, link row, small columns and a giant lime wordmark at the bottom.

## Motion
- Calm fade-up on everything: y 20px, 0.9s tween, ease [0.22,1,0.36,1], 0.1s delay, at 20% in view (31 effects).
- Hotel cards use position sticky to stack.
- Gallery ticker at velocity 40, barely slowing on hover.
- No parallax; energy comes from scale of type rather than motion.

## Phone
Wordmark scales to fit the width at the top of the hero. Headlines drop to 42 to 58px with the lime rule kept. Hotel cards become image-over-text stacks; service cards stack with big numerals; the experience table turns into stacked rows with the tag under the title. Nav becomes logo plus menu and a lime CTA.

## Signature moments
- Lime rule pushing the second headline line.
- Full-width lowercase wordmark on a film photo.
- Sticky numbered hotel cards.
- Experience table with category tags.

## Borrow
- One grotesk family at 500 with heavy negative tracking for a modern luxury voice.
- A drawn rule inside the headline as a brand device.
- Numbered table rows for experiences or services instead of cards.
- Cool palette with a single acid accent reserved for actions.
- Sticky stacking cards for a short list of locations or products.

## Avoid
- Lime on white has poor contrast for text; use it only as fill or graphic.
- The two-line headline with rule is used in almost every section; vary it for longer pages.
- Very tight -0.055em tracking breaks at small sizes; loosen below 40px.
- Mist blue bands next to grainy grey photos can look washed out on low quality images.
