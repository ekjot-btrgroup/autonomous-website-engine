# Beanro  (hospitality-restaurants)
A café and coffee-brand homepage that sells drinks like products. Everything is warm and edible: cream paper, roast-brown type, an orange that reads as caramel. The voice is loud and cheerful, carried by a very wide caps display face, sticker-like tags and drinks shot as clean cut-outs.

## Type
- Display: Boldonse 400, uppercase, ultra-wide. H1 70px / 150% / -4px (46, 36); H2 48px / -2px (30, 24); slab headline 72px; footer wordmark 140px (110, 88). The wide letters make two lines fill the 1200px measure.
- Secondary heads and card titles: Bayon 400 condensed caps, 26 to 54px, tight tracking; this contrast between wide and narrow caps is the whole type idea.
- Body: Manrope 400 to 700, 16 to 20px, 140 to 170% leading; small labels in Manrope semibold uppercase, often in the burnt orange-brown.
- Prices in Bayon, sitting low left on each card.

## Colour
- Cream #f9eedc page with a lighter #fcf7ee for panels; a darker cream for product cards so they sit inside the panel.
- Roast brown #7d2c0f for headlines and the big slab; cocoa #664237 for secondary text and active tab pills.
- Orange #ff8342 for the order button, links, star icons and the boxed highlight phrase.
- Sticker colours used sparingly: mustard #fed16a, sage #485f4e, pale peach #ffefd5.
- No shadows; depth comes from cream-on-cream tonal steps.

## Layout and spacing
- 1440px canvas, 60px side padding, 160px between sections.
- Big rounded panels (32px and 60px radius) hold the menu and the slab; cards use 16 to 24px radius; buttons and tabs are full pills.
- Centred hero and slab, split heads elsewhere (headline left, short uppercase blurb right).
- Thin vertical rules divide the hero background into five columns, a quiet structural texture.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: announcement bar, centred nav with logo in the middle and an orange pill order button; two-line wide caps headline; a large cut-out liquid splash under it over faint column lines.
2. About: tag pill, then a centred multi-line statement in wide caps with small inline images of a cup and a bean placed between words.
3. Menu panel: split head, pill tabs for drink categories, 3 by 2 grid of drink cards (rating top right, cut-out drink, condensed name, price and text link).
4. Feature video: centred heading, then a pinned video that scales up from half size as you scroll, with short claims pinned left and right.
5. Slab: rounded roast-brown panel with a four-line headline, one phrase boxed and tilted in orange, four rotated sticker pills, short centred paragraph.
6. Three numbered value cards with circled numbers and small heads.
7. Testimonials: split head, a slideshow of quote cards and a ticker.
8. Story: split block with an image that resolves from blur on scroll, heading, paragraph and orange pill button.
9. FAQ: split head and an accordion of rounded rows with orange arrow icons.
10. CTA: drink cut-outs on both sides of a wide caps headline set on a brown band, pill button; then a full-width orange-brown footer ending in a huge wordmark.

## Motion
- Hero headline lines slide in from x -250px with opacity, spring stiffness 100, damping 30, on mount.
- Sticker pills rock with a 3s linear mirror loop to -30 degrees rotation.
- Feature video scales from 0.5 to 1.5 against a scroll target while two side claims stay sticky.
- Story image switches from a blurred variant to sharp when 50% in view.
- Most sections fade up once on appear (18 appear effects in total).

## Phone
Single column at 390px. Wide caps H1 drops to 36px and wraps to four lines; the splash sits under it. Menu grid becomes a single stack of drink cards; tabs scroll in one row. The slab keeps its stickers but they overlap the text more. Footer wordmark stays edge to edge.

## Signature moments
- Wide caps headline over a floating liquid splash.
- Brown slab with the boxed orange phrase and rotating sticker tags.
- Cream-on-cream product cards with isolated drinks.
- Inline mini images inside a big caps sentence.

## Borrow
- Pairing one ultra-wide display face with one condensed face for instant personality in food and drink brands.
- Product cut-outs on tonal cards for any menu, shop or catalogue.
- A single high-energy slab with tilted sticker tags to break a calm page.
- Thin vertical column rules as hero texture instead of a photo.

## Avoid
- Uppercase everywhere, including body labels, tires the eye; keep running copy in sentence case.
- The scroll-scaled video section is empty in static captures and very tall; give it a poster frame.
- Ratings and prices on the cards are template figures; only show real ones.
- Stickers placed over headline letters can hurt legibility on phone.
