# Travelo  (hospitality-travel)
A tour operator and travel agency site for companies selling packaged trips to many destinations. It balances trust and wanderlust: a crisp ice-blue and navy system with an elegant tightly tracked serif feels established and calm, while big saturated aerial photography and a giant wordmark give the first screen poster energy.

## Type
- Display: Spectral 400 with strong negative tracking. H1 90px / 0.96 / -0.064em (78, 54); hero uses H1 S 64px; H2 56px / -0.06em (46, 38); H2 S 50px; H3 42px; card titles 22 to 25px serif.
- Body: Golos Text around 400 to 410, 15 to 20px, slightly negative tracking, 1.35 to 1.45 leading.
- Tags: Golos 13 to 14px uppercase, +0.09em, centred between two thin arrow rules.
- Wordmark: a wide geometric techno face set at full container width in white.
- Prices in Golos medium, right aligned against the serif title.

## Colour
- Ice blue #f0f7fc for most sections; white for alternating bands and all cards.
- Deep navy #0a1345 for the featured destinations and stats bands; royal navy #1b2978 for headings on light, button arrow squares and feature cards.
- An orange token exists but is almost unused; the page reads blue and white.
- Hairlines at 7 to 10% black; one border only, no shadows.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px canvas, 32px side padding, 100 to 120px vertical padding.
- Centred section heads (tag, two-line serif H2) for most sections, split layouts for promise and process.
- Cards: white with 20px radius and an inner image with 16px radius inset by about 14px, then title and price row, hairline, location and rating row.
- Buttons: white pill with navy label and an inset navy square containing a diagonal arrow; inverse on dark.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed aerial seascape with a slow zoom; two-line serif H1 and paragraph left, hairline, white pill button and a social proof chip with stacked avatars; a floating white tour card on the right; the brand name as a giant wide wordmark across the bottom.
2. About: tag, large word-revealing statement in serif, button, social proof and three small about cards with images.
3. Featured destinations (navy): centred head, a wide slideshow of destination photos with labels and arrows, button.
4. Why us: centred head, a central portrait photo with four feature blurbs around it that slide in from the sides as you scroll.
5. Our promise: split head, then four navy cards that fly across a long pinned track as you scroll.
6. Tours: centred head, 3 by 2 grid of tour cards with category chip, title, price, location and rating, centred button.
7. Testimonials: centred head and a ticker of quote cards.
8. Our approach: split layout with numbered step tabs on the left driving a pinned column of image cards on the right.
9. Stats (navy): heading and four big numbers with decorative vector lines.
10. Blog: three article cards.
11. FAQ: heading, small advisor photo and button on the left, accordion on the right.
12. Social ticker: heading and an image ticker before the closing photo CTA and footer.

## Motion
- Hero image settles from scale 1.1 on mount with a long soft spring (1.8s, bounce 0.1), then loops a 14s linear zoom to 1.07 and back.
- Headings reveal word by word: y 20px with 5px blur, spring stiffness 200 and damping 80.
- Scroll-target transforms slide feature blurbs in by about 196px; scroll variants switch step tabs; three pinned stages.
- Ticker rows for testimonials and the social strip; smooth scroll.

## Phone
Single column. Hero H1 at 54px, the tour card moves below the copy and the wordmark scales to full width. Tour grid becomes a stack of cards; pinned promise and approach sections become plain stacks. Nav collapses to logo and menu icon.

## Signature moments
- Floating tour card on the hero photo.
- Giant wide wordmark with a registered mark.
- Arrow-flanked centred tags.
- Pill button with the navy arrow square.

## Borrow
- A real product card (tour, room, listing) floating in the hero to make the offer concrete above the fold.
- Very tight tracked serif headings with a neutral sans body for a trustworthy travel or service brand.
- Inset image cards (outer white, inner rounded image) for listings.
- Alternating ice blue, white and navy bands to pace a long page.

## Avoid
- Too many scroll-pinned sections (promise, approach, why us) make the page very long and render as empty space in static captures.
- Section heads are almost all centred two-line serif; vary alignment.
- The techno wordmark font clashes with the serif voice if reused outside the logo.
- Placeholder trust numbers in stats; only use real figures in client work.
