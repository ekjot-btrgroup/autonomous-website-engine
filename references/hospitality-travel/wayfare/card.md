# Wayfare  (hospitality-travel)
A small-group tour operator homepage that feels like a well-kept travel journal. Warm sand paper, near-black ink and one sun-yellow accent sit under grainy, golden-hour film photography. The page breathes between calm typographic sections and large rounded photo windows that hold the moments of drama.

## Type
- Display: Clash Grotesk 500. Display 88px / 1.0 / -0.03em (66, 44); H1 and XL 64px / 1.05; H2 44px / 1.1 / -0.025em (38, 30); H3 26px; H4 21px; stats 48px; quote style 34px at weight 400.
- Body: General Sans 400, lead 18px / 1.55, body 16px / 1.6, small 15px; labels and chips 13 to 15px medium.
- Eyebrows: General Sans 13px uppercase, +0.08em, muted taupe, above every section head.
- Footer wordmark: Clash Grotesk 600 at 280px / 0.9 / -0.05em in pale foam on near-black, cropped by the page bottom.

## Colour
- Sand #f5efe4 page, lighter surface #fcf9f3 for cards and inputs, shell #ece3d3 for chips.
- Ink #211d16 for type and dark buttons; night #25211a for the dark panel and footer with its own line colour.
- Sun yellow #ffc94a only on primary buttons, rating stars and small dots; yellow buttons use a dark brown label.
- Hairlines #e0d6c4. Full dark theme defined for every token.
- Almost no shadows; one soft shadow on the floating nav, glass blur behind it.

## Layout and spacing
- 1440px canvas with 70px side padding and 112px vertical rhythm; statement and intro sections stay airy with lots of sand.
- Photo windows are the key device: a section gets 12px padding and a 24 to 28px rounded panel fills it, so the image never touches the viewport edge.
- Section heads split: eyebrow and heading left, short paragraph or text link right.
- Cards 24px radius, chips and buttons full pills; primary button is a yellow pill with a pale round arrow chip inside.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero window: inset photo of a coastal cliff, floating pill nav at the top centre (logo, links, language, theme toggle, yellow button), chip with a dot, two-line display headline bottom left, lead and two pill buttons.
2. Statement: a large three-line sentence that fills with ink as you scroll.
3. Intro: small copy and button left with an avatar stack and rating, tall photo centre, a ruled table of key facts right with a short note.
4. Destinations: header with text link, then a draggable ticker of tall rounded destination photos with names underneath.
5. Trips: head, a pill-shaped filter bar with two selects and a clear button, 3 by 2 grid of all-photo trip cards, dark pill to see all.
6. Travel styles window: dark panel, split head, tall photo left, five large numbered rows right; hovering a row brightens it and changes the photo.
7. How it works: a photo of a map and notebook left, three numbered steps right with hairlines, text link.
8. Quote window: inset photo of a sail at sunset with a five-star row and a large quote bottom left.
9. Reviews: head and two ticker rows of review cards moving in opposite directions with faded edges.
10. Included: head, large photo left, 2 by 2 grid of included items with icon tiles right.
11. Journal: head with a pill link, three article cards with image, category chip, date and title.
12. FAQ: head, short text and button left, accordion of six questions right.
13. CTA window: inset dusk photo with a centred heading, two buttons and an image trail that follows the cursor; then a closing signup row and a dark footer with the giant cropped wordmark.

## Motion
- Appear: y 14px and fade, tween 0.9s with ease [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1], 0.05s delay; 26 appear effects.
- Headings reveal word by word: y 26px, 8px blur, 1s tween with the same ease, 0.04s per word.
- Card hover dims to 0.88 opacity over 0.5s; style list rows swap photos on hover.
- Destination ticker at 34 px/s, slows to 35% on hover and can be dragged; reviews tick in two rows.
- Statement text fills on scroll; cursor image trail in the CTA; smooth scrolling.

## Phone
Single column at 390px with the 12px window inset kept. Display headline drops to 44px. Floating nav becomes a compact pill with logo and menu button. Trip cards stack full width; the travel styles list sits under its photo; ticker rows stay horizontal. Footer wordmark shrinks to 84px.

## Signature moments
- Inset rounded photo windows with the floating nav.
- All-photo trip cards with chips and yellow pill buttons.
- Dark numbered hover list that swaps the image.
- Scroll-filled statement sentence.

## Borrow
- The 12px inset window for every full-bleed image: instant polish and a calm frame.
- One warm accent used only on actions, against sand and near-black.
- Filmic, slightly faded photography graded to one warm tone across the page.
- Split section heads with eyebrow, heading and a short side note.
- A dark hover list to present service categories in a compact, interactive way.

## Avoid
- Rating badges, review counts, trip prices and facts in the intro table are template figures; use only real data.
- Four photo windows plus two tickers make a long page; drop one window for smaller brands.
- Muted taupe small text on sand is low contrast; darken it for body copy.
