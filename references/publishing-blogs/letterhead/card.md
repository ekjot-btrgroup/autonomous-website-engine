# Letterhead  (publishing-blogs)
A personal newsletter for a single writer that wants to feel like a well-made book rather than a SaaS landing page. Everything is one serif, the colours are pale greens and paper whites, and the dreamy painted landscapes make the subscription feel like a calm weekly ritual.

## Type
- EB Garamond only, always weight 400 (500 for buttons and logo). H1 76px, tracking -0.025em, leading 1.08 (60 tablet, 46 phone); display 64px; H2 52px; H3 28px.
- Body L 21px and body 18px at -0.02em tracking with generous 1.55 to 1.6 leading: reading comfort is the point.
- Italic does all the small work: issue meta (number, date, read time) in green italic, labels in muted italic, a 30px italic signature under the intro letter.
- Numerals stay in the serif (60px numbers, 40px stats), giving stats a literary rather than dashboard feel.

## Colour
- White ground, pale green-grey surface (#f3f6f2) for every tile and card, hairlines #dce3de.
- Ink #14201a, body #1f2622, muted #4d5650.
- One accent, forest green #2f5d46: subscribe buttons, the featured pricing card fill, lit cells in the source grid, meta text.
- Pastel tints (sky #dceaf2, peach #f7e4d8, leaf #e3eedb) appear only inside micro-graphics and the painted banners.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px, 40px gutters, 120px section padding; most sections open with a left-aligned H2 and a one-line intro.
- 24px radius on all cards, banners and the hero card; no shadows, no borders on tiles; pill-shaped inputs and buttons.
- Sticky top nav: logo left, three text links centred, green pill subscribe right.
- Hero and closing CTA are inset rounded banners filled with a painted landscape that whitens toward the top so dark serif type sits on it cleanly.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: rounded painted banner, centred two-line serif headline, one-sentence promise, pill email field joined to a green subscribe pill, italic social proof line beneath.
2. Logos: italic "read by people at" line and a greyed logo ticker.
3. About: two cards side by side: a letter from the writer (paragraphs, topic chips, italic signature) and a pull-quote card plus a ruled "inside every issue" list with times.
4. Features (bento): a 2-up row then a 3-up row of tiles, each with a short serif title, one line, and a tiny illustration: segmented reading-time bar, dotted source grid, illustrated rug, a white "try this week" note, a globe with a percentage.
5. Recent issues: heading and outline button left; stacked issue cards right with a small painted thumbnail, italic meta line and two-line title.
6. Banner: a second painted landscape with a centred line and green button.
7. Writer: portrait photo left, "hi I'm" serif heading, two paragraphs and three serif stats right.
8. Pricing: heading with a monthly/yearly segmented toggle; three plan cards, the third filled forest green with white text and a circular badge.
9. Reader notes: big serif count and heading above rows of small pill-shaped reader quotes drifting horizontally.
10. Final CTA: painted banner, centred two-line headline, subscribe form.
11. Footer: logo, short blurb, email, link columns, then a huge pale-grey serif wordmark across the bottom.

## Motion
- Quiet: 10 appear effects, each a 12px fade-up, tween 0.5s, ease [0.22,1,0.36,1], triggered on view with no replay.
- Logo row and reader-note pills move as slow tickers; everything else is still.

## Phone
Single column; hero headline 46px in four lines; bento tiles stack; issue cards keep thumbnail plus text in a row; pricing cards stack with the featured green card last; the footer wordmark shrinks but stays full width.

## Signature moments
- The painted misty landscapes behind serif headlines.
- Bento tiles that explain value with tiny, specific graphics instead of icons.
- The forest-green featured plan among pale cards.
- The oversized pale serif wordmark closing the page.

## Borrow
- Single-serif system with italic for meta and labels: instant editorial warmth.
- Micro-graphic tiles (bars, dot grids, a globe) to make abstract benefits concrete.
- Inset rounded image banners as soft section breaks.
- Pill reader quotes in drifting rows as lightweight social proof.

## Avoid
- Painted AI landscapes can look generic if reused; commission or choose imagery that fits the brand.
- Long white gaps after some bento rows; tighten when adapting.
- Section crops in this capture are offset upward by roughly the nav height (sticky nav), so the hero crop starts mid-headline; use the full-page thumb for the hero.
