# Memoir  (publishing-blogs)
A personal publishing site for a writer or creator who sells newsletters and paid letters. It borrows the structure of a reading app (fixed sidebar, search, filter, card grid) and softens it with a warm greige palette and one italic serif word in each headline, so it feels personal and premium rather than corporate.

## Type
- Manrope 500 for display: H1 64px, tracking -0.06em, leading 1.1 (56 tablet, 40 phone); H2 40px at -0.06em. Very tight tracking gives the grotesk an editorial density.
- One word per headline is replaced with Source Serif 4 italic (for example the adjective or the possessive), the main expressive device of the site.
- Body and UI in Manrope 500 to 600 at 12 to 18px with -0.04em tracking; paragraph text 18px with 1.8 leading.
- Tiny uppercase 10px labels with +0.13em tracking for sidebar group titles.

## Colour
- Warm neutral scale: background 50 (#f4f2f0), 100 (#edeae7) for inputs and the sidebar, 200 (#dad3ce) for hairlines, 500 to 700 browns for muted meta.
- White cards on the greige page; black for headings and the solid subscribe pill buttons.
- No saturated accent: warmth comes from the photography (terracotta, oak, cream).

## Layout and spacing
- Fixed 240px left sidebar on desktop, separated by tone (100) rather than a border; main column centred in the remaining width.
- Hero centred: small pill eyebrow, two-line headline, email field and black button side by side, a tiny reassurance line.
- Toolbar row: wide search input left, category select right, both pill-rounded in tone 100 with hairlines.
- Post grid: 3 columns, white cards with 12px radius and 12px inner padding, image about 4:3 with 10px radius, two-line title, small arrow chip, category and read time meta.
- Mission CTA: big rounded white-ish panel with pill eyebrow, two-line headline (again with one italic word) and the same subscribe pair.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Sidebar (sticky): avatar, name and role, collapse toggle; nav items with icons and a count badge; social links with arrow glyphs; account link; pinned post card; mini subscribe input.
2. Hero: eyebrow pill, headline with italic serif word, inline subscribe form and note.
3. Letters: search plus category filter, 3x2 grid of post cards, load more.
4. Mission CTA: large soft panel with headline and subscribe pair.
5. Footer: centred italic serif wordmark, two rows of small links, credit line.

## Motion
- Content rises 48px from transparent on mount, spring 0.5s with no bounce, 0.2s delay; five such reveals.
- Sidebar is sticky; smooth scroll is on site-wide. Otherwise still.

## Phone
Sidebar becomes a bottom-fixed icon tab bar (home, about, letters, social); headline 40px in three lines; filter and search stack; cards become a single column with full-width images; subscribe button goes full width under the field.

## Signature moments
- The italic serif word inside a tight sans headline.
- Sidebar profile that makes a blog feel like an app.
- A single, consistent warm photo grade across all thumbnails.

## Borrow
- Mixed-font headline accent (sans with one serif italic word).
- App-style sidebar or bottom tab bar for content-led personal brands.
- Greige tonal layering (50/100/200) instead of borders and shadows.
- Subscribe pair repeated at top and bottom with identical styling.

## Avoid
- Very short homepage; for a client site add an about or offer section.
- Floating template button (use template for free) is not part of the design.
- Capture note: the homepage is one wrapper frame, so only one useful section crop exists; use the thumb for detail.
