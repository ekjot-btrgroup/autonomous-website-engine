# Kendi  (publishing-magazines)
A general-interest online magazine or multi-author blog that looks current and neutral so the photography carries the mood. A pale grey page with white cards, black Inter type and a classic main-column-plus-sidebar layout make it feel like a real publication rather than a portfolio.

## Type
- Inter only. Display 72px weight 600, tracking -0.03em, leading 0.95 (58 tablet, 48 phone); section titles 34px 600; card titles 16 to 20px 600 with the same tight tracking.
- Paragraphs 14 to 16px weight 500 at -0.02em, leading 1.4, so even body text feels firm.
- Meta line pattern on every card: tiny round author avatar, author name, category, dot separator and date in 12px.
- Category chips: small white or black rectangles with 12px labels sitting on image corners.

## Colour
- Background #f5f5f5, white cards, black ink, grey #3d3d3d for secondary text, #dedede hairlines.
- No accent colour at all: buttons are solid black with white text and a small arrow; secondary buttons white. Colour comes only from photos.
- Black overlays at 30 to 80 percent on image cards to keep white titles legible.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px with 30px gutters. Hero row: headline block left, intro text plus email field right.
- Below the hero: full-width featured story (about 16:6) with chip top-left and title bottom-left, followed by a row of three small horizontal post cards (thumb plus title plus meta) on white.
- Feed: 740px main column of blocks (title then list), sidebar about 400px on the right, 100px between blocks.
- Radii small (5 to 10px), almost no shadows, one border style.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: eyebrow, two-line display headline, intro and inline subscribe on the right; featured story slider; three compact story cards.
2. Editor's pick: large primary card (image, chip, avatar meta, title, excerpt) beside a stack of four small thumb-and-title rows.
3. Sponsored: centred tiny label, wide dark ad banner with product photo, headline and white button.
4. Trending: two primary cards side by side, then a 2x2 of compact cards.
5. Inspiration: slider of two dark image cards with chips and white titles, round arrow controls top-right.
6. Latest posts: list rows of image left (about 4:3), meta, title and two-line excerpt right, plus a black "view more" button.
7. Sidebar (running beside 2 to 6): about card with logo and social icons, 2x3 grid of image topic tiles with centred labels, newsletter card with consent checkbox, numbered spotlight list with thumbnails, sponsored product card.
8. Newsletter band: three tilted overlapping photos above a centred heading, line, email field and consent row.
9. Footer: logo and blurb, navigation and category columns, a 2x2 image grid, legal row.

## Motion
- Essentially static: no appear, scroll or text effects recorded. Movement only from the hero slideshow and the inspiration slider controls.

## Phone
Single column: headline 48px, subscribe field full width, featured story keeps its overlay title; sidebar content drops below the feed; topic tiles become a 2-column grid; list rows stack image above text.

## Signature moments
- The confident tight-tracked display headline next to an inline subscribe box.
- The sidebar's image-tile topic grid.
- Three fanned, slightly rotated photos above the newsletter call.

## Borrow
- Main column plus sidebar for content-heavy sites (blog, news, resources hubs).
- A consistent meta row (avatar, author, category, date) on every card.
- Sponsored slots styled as native dark banners rather than generic ad boxes.
- No-accent palette that lets editorial photography provide colour.

## Avoid
- Homepage is long and dense; trim to three feed blocks when adapting.
- Without motion it can feel flat; add light reveals if the brand allows.
- Template promo buttons (remix, more templates) in the hero are not part of the design.
- Capture note: only the hero crop is correct; crops 02 to 06 are mispositioned because those blocks sit inside a nested main column, so use the full-page thumb for them.
