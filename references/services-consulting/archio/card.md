# Archio  (services-consulting)
A boutique business consultancy site that trades the usual corporate blue for paper tones and a deep forest green. A thin, tightly tracked serif carries every headline, which makes the advice feel considered and a little literary while the sans body keeps it practical.

## Type
- Display: Crimson Pro 300, h1 70px / -0.04em / leading 1.0 (54px phone); h2 44px / 1.1 (38px tablet and phone); secondary h2 36px.
- Card titles: Inter 500 24-36px with slight negative tracking, so card content reads as UI against serif section titles.
- Body: Inter 400 18px / 1.5 / -0.02em in 70% ink; small body 15px / 1.6; labels Satoshi 500 14px with +0.8px tracking.
- Hierarchy is serif-vs-sans contrast plus size; weights stay light at the top.

## Colour
- White and warm stone #ecebe4 bands alternate on every section; cards sit on #f6f4ed with soft inner glow.
- Forest green #0e3a27 for primary pill buttons (with a drop shadow), table winner header and stars.
- Pale sage #e6eecd for secondary buttons, giving a two-tone button pair everywhere.
- Black text at 85/70/50% steps; a bright green dot used once for the availability badge.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px frame, 50px gutters, 100px vertical padding on every band, centered heading plus 2-line lead above each block.
- Cards are 16-18px radius, almost borderless, separated by a faint shadow; photos inside are 10-12px radius.
- Pill radius (1000px) for buttons, nav and badges. Decorative scribble line art and soft circle shapes float behind white sections.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: left column serif headline, short lead, green and sage buttons, hairline, star rating row. Right: two tall rounded portraits side by side with an avatar stack and client count under a hairline. Floating pill nav sits under the hero.
2. Services: stone band, 2x2 large cards, each with icon tile, title, copy and two photos; a centered CTA card below.
3. Why us: three feature cards in a slideshow with icons, then a trust row of check badges between hairlines.
4. Process: sticky left column with heading, buttons and rating; right column stack of three big numbered step cards.
5. Comparison table: three columns, generic option with dashes, the offer column with a green header and checks.
6. Testimonials: ticker carousel of quote cards, then three big stat numbers and a button pair.
7. Blog: two article cards with large image, title, excerpt and read-more pill.
8. FAQ: dark quote card with a serif pull quote on the left, accordion on the right.
9. Team: three portrait cards with name, role and profile link pill.
10. Footer: giant serif wordmark spanning the page width, centered tagline and pill link row.

## Motion
- Text effect on headings: word-by-word, y 10px plus blur 10px, spring stiffness 400 / damping 100, 0.05s step, delay 0.5s on mount.
- Appear: linear fade 0.6s with 0.8s delay at 50% in view (simple opacity, no travel).
- Hero portraits on parallax speed 105; sticky process column; fixed blur gradient at the bottom of the viewport; smooth scroll.
- Tickers for service images and testimonials.

## Phone
- Hero stacks: headline, buttons, then the two portraits side by side; nav pill stays floating.
- Cards go single column; the comparison table keeps three narrow columns.
- h1 drops to 54px, h2 to 38px.

## Signature moments
- Light serif headlines with tight tracking on a stone background.
- Forest-green and sage button pair repeated after almost every section.
- Full-width serif wordmark footer.

## Borrow
- Service card with an icon tile and a two-photo strip: shows breadth without a gallery section.
- Comparison table with one tinted winning column for any advisory or service offer.
- Bottom-docked floating nav plus fixed blur gradient for a calm, app-like frame.
- Word-blur text reveal for headings only, keep everything else as plain fades.

## Avoid
- Repeating the same button pair and trust-badge row after nearly every section feels padded.
- Too many centered heading plus lead blocks in a row; vary alignment.
- Stock-like photography and the odd sculpture shot weaken the premium tone.
