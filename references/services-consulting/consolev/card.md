# CONSOLEV  (services-consulting)
A boutique strategy and advisory firm site that sells calm authority. Ultra-light, tightly tracked grotesk headlines with a single italic serif word give it an editorial, considered voice; a warm stone canvas alternates with inset near-black, cream and forest panels, and numbered caps labels make the page read like a well-organised report.

## Type
- Display: Inter Display 300, 88px, tracking -0.045em, leading 0.96 (64 / 46). Three lines, last word swapped to Instrument Serif italic.
- H2: Inter Display 300, 56px, tracking -0.035em, leading 1.02 (44 / 34); every H2 ends with an italic serif phrase.
- Lead: Inter Display 300, 36px for newsletter and intro statements.
- Quotes: Instrument Serif italic 25 to 34px, leading 1.18 to 1.25.
- Body Large 18px and Body 16px in Inter; Label 11px 500 caps with 0.14em tracking, written as (01) NAME.
- Hierarchy comes from very light weights at large sizes versus small dense caps labels.

## Colour
- Canvas #eeebe5 warm stone; Surface #f7f5f0 cream for light panels.
- Ink #121210 for text; Muted #6e6b64 for descriptions.
- Night #0f0f0e and Night Card #1a1a18 for the principles panel; Forest #1c2a21 for testimonials.
- Sage #cfddb8 only in the hero progress bars and tiny accents. Lines at 12% ink.
- Buttons are cream or glass pills with a small dot before the label.

## Layout and spacing
- 1136px content with 32px side padding; section padding a generous 150px.
- Big moments are full-width rounded panels (24 to 32px radius) inset 12px from the edges: hero photo, dark principles, cream framework, forest testimonials, CTA landscape.
- Centred intro statement over a logo ticker; elsewhere a left label plus heading and right content split.
- Services as ruled list rows: small numeral, big light title, grey description, outline pill.
- Framework uses a sticky heading column left and a 2-column grid of numbered cards right.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: inset photo card, three-line light headline with italic last word, grey copy, two dot pills, a frosted card with three labelled progress bars; a hairline and three spaced caps statements along the bottom.
2. Intro: numbered label, centred two-line statement with italic tail, short copy, then a ticker of muted client wordmarks.
3. Principles (night panel): heading with italic second line, four dark cards with italic serif numerals, title and text.
4. Founder: large circular portrait with a pill name tag, heading, two paragraphs, an italic serif pull quote and a pill link.
5. Services: label and heading row with pill, four ruled rows with numerals and Explore pills.
6. Framework (cream panel): sticky left heading, six numbered cards in two columns.
7. Case studies: three image cards with caps category chips and short titles.
8. How we work: centred heading, three ruled columns with numerals and titles.
9. Testimonials (forest panel): misty forest photo, centred heading, three frosted glass quote cards in serif italic.
10. FAQ: heading left with a small photo and pill, accordion right.
11. CTA: coastal landscape panel, centred white heading with italic phrase, pill button.
12. Newsletter: lead-size line with italic tail, email field and dark pill.
Footer: night panel with link columns and a giant wordmark over a landscape strip.

## Motion
- Hero image settles from scale 1.12 over a 2.2s spring with no bounce.
- Headline words rise 28px from a 10px blur, 1.1s spring, 0.15s delay.
- Hero glass card floats 10px up and down, 3.5s mirror loop.
- Logo ticker at velocity 40, slowing on hover; case images zoom to 1.06 on hover over a 0.9s spring.
- 44 appear effects; sticky framework heading.

## Phone
- Panels keep 12px gutters; hero glass card moves below the copy.
- Principles, framework and case cards stack single column; service rows keep numeral and title, description underneath.
- Display 46px, H2 34px; nav is a wordmark plus menu pill.

## Signature moments
- Light grotesk plus one italic serif word in every heading.
- Numbered caps section labels.
- Frosted quote cards over a misty forest.
- Floating progress card on the hero.

## Borrow
- Weight 300 display type for quiet confidence instead of bold claims.
- (01) style numbered labels as a navigation rhythm.
- Mixing inset panels of three tones (night, cream, forest) to pace a long page.
- Ruled service list with oversized light titles.

## Avoid
- The italic serif trick on every heading becomes predictable; reserve it for key lines.
- Demo testimonials and client names must be replaced with real ones or removed.
- Thin 300 weight at small sizes loses legibility; keep it for 36px and above.
