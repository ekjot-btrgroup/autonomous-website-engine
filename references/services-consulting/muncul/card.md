# Muncul  (services-consulting)
A financial strategy and advisory firm site. Calm, businesslike base (warm off-white, near-black type, plenty of white cards) lifted by one sharp chartreuse used on buttons, ticks and stars, plus a deep green for button caps. Dark slabs (hero, pricing, contact) punctuate a light page so the rhythm reads light, dark, light.

## Type
- Display: Inter Display 500, 80px, tracking -0.02em, leading 1.1 (64 / 48). Two short lines, left aligned, white on the dark hero.
- H2: Inter Display 500, 52px (46 / 40), left aligned under a small uppercase label.
- H5 and H6: 24px and 20px for card titles and quotes.
- Body: Inter 16 to 18px, -0.02em, leading 1.4, grey #616161; 12px uppercase labels; 14px menu.

## Colour
- Background #fafaf7 with soft gradients into white between sections; Soft Background #f5f5e9 for inset trays.
- Ink #242424 headings, #000000 for dark slabs and CTA tiles.
- Chartreuse #b4e717 for primary buttons, star ratings and check accents.
- Dark green #1c4b42 for the circular arrow cap inside buttons and check badges.
- Hairline borders at 45% grey; no shadows.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 frame, 30px side padding, 100px vertical padding per section.
- Headers sit left: label, H2, two-line grey lead; the section body sits below.
- Cards 20px radius, buttons and nav fully rounded.
- Large content groups sit inside a pale tray with 10px inner gap so cards look inset.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: darkened full-bleed skyline photo, logo left, white pill nav centred, chartreuse contact pill right; two-line white headline, short lead, chartreuse button and text link; slide dots on the right; service tabs peeking at the bottom edge.
2. Clients: small heading and a reversed ticker of client logo cards.
3. Services: label and H2, three-column grid of photo cards with title and short text, button below.
4. About: white section with heading and copy left, a large video or photo tile right, then four stat cards in a row.
5. Process: four step cards (step number top right, title, text at the bottom) in a cream tray, then a wide photo tile with logo and a black "ready" tile with button.
6. Case studies: a featured case card with photo, figures and quote, then a list of secondary case rows.
7. Testimonials: video testimonial tile and three quote cards with ratings.
8. Pricing: black rounded slab with label, H2, avatar and rating proof, segmented plan toggle, a plan summary card and a wide inclusions card with price and button; a closing line with a named contact and a second button.
9. FAQ: white section with heading and a categorised question list.
10. Blog: three post cards.
Footer area (from the layout template): dark contact section with a form, then a light footer with link columns.

## Motion
- Headings reveal word by word: 40px rise on a spring (stiffness 200, damping 40), 8 text effects.
- Blocks rise 40px in view on a spring (stiffness 200, damping 30), 21 appear effects.
- Client logo ticker runs in reverse at velocity 20; pricing tabs slide between plans.

## Phone
- Hero headline drops to 48px; nav collapses to a hamburger.
- Grids stack to one column; step cards and stat cards stack.
- Pricing slab keeps its black panel with cards stacked.

## Signature moments
- Dark photo hero with a floating white pill nav.
- Chartreuse button with a dark green arrow cap.
- Black pricing slab with tabbed plans.

## Borrow
- Inset tray holding step cards plus a photo tile and a CTA tile in one block.
- Light, dark, light rhythm using full-width rounded slabs.
- Segmented plan toggle instead of three side by side price columns.

## Avoid
- Hero photo is very dark; keep enough contrast without crushing the image.
- Client counts, ratings, prices and figures are template placeholders; never reuse invented proof.
- The Framer remix badge and template helper visible in the screenshot are not part of the design.
