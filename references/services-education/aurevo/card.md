# Aurevo  (services-education)
A personal-brand coach or creator educator selling a ladder of offers (course, bootcamp, community, one to one). Clean near-white page with charcoal type and a loud electric lime used as a frame colour, not just a button. Busy and conversion-led: proof chips, avatars, results numbers and lead magnets appear in nearly every section.

## Type
- One family throughout: Open Sauce One.
- H1: 700, 64px, leading 1.2 (46 / 34). Four short stacked lines, left aligned, charcoal.
- H2: 700, 46px, leading 1.3 (38 / 26), usually two centred lines with a grey two-line lead under it.
- H3: 700, 32px for offer titles, with a small blue line icon in front.
- Body: 400, 18px, leading 1.6; 14px for chips, captions and card meta.
- Big result figures in case studies at about 40px bold, blue on lime and lime on charcoal.

## Colour
- Page #fafafa, cards white, charcoal #333333 for text, buttons and dark frames.
- Lime #cefe1e as a thick card frame, hero tag fill and logo disc.
- Electric blue #374cff only for small icons and figures on lime.
- Muted sage green panel (a photo texture) for the free resources card in the hero.
- Greys #757575 and #b0b0b0 for secondary copy; thin #cccccc outlines on chips.

## Layout and spacing
- About 1100px content; sections sit on the plain page with around 100px vertical padding.
- Card radius 30px outer, 20px inner; frames are 24px thick so the inner white panel and photo read as inset.
- Pill buttons in charcoal with a white circular arrow cap on the right.
- Outlined grey pill chips for tags inside cards.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: three columns. Left: lime tag pill with trend icon, four-line H1, grey lead, charcoal pill button, small "as seen in" logo row. Centre: tall rounded portrait with frosted topic chips at the bottom. Right: star rating line, avatar stack, short quote with link, then a sage card listing two free resources with icon tiles.
2. About: heading with a photo reveal (blinds effect), bio copy, social icons and three number cards.
3. Services: centred header, four large offer cards stacked one after another, alternating lime and charcoal frames; each has a white panel (icon title, copy, "what to expect" chips, button) and a photo on the right. Cards stick and shrink as the next arrives.
4. Testimonials: centred header and a ticker of review cards.
5. Success stories: sticky left column with heading and a small CTA card; right column of four case study cards alternating lime and charcoal, each with avatar, name, offer tag, title, white quote panel and two big result figures.
6. Content: centred header and a row of three video thumbnails with play buttons and view counts.
7. Free resources: two lead magnet cards (charcoal and lime) with checklists and buttons.
8. FAQ: sticky heading left, accordion right.
Footer area (from the layout template): contact block with form and a short CTA, then a lime-accent footer with link columns.

## Motion
- Blocks rise 50px in view with a spring (stiffness 300, damping 60), 22 appear effects.
- Offer cards are sticky and scale to 0.6 with a slight tilt and fade as the next card covers them (3 scroll transforms).
- Sticky left columns in case studies and FAQ; blinds reveal on the about photo; testimonial ticker; smooth scroll; fixed header.

## Phone
- Hero stacks: headline, button, portrait, then proof and resources card.
- Offer card interiors stack photo under the white panel; frames stay.
- Case study figures move under the quote; sticky columns become normal flow.

## Signature moments
- Lime frame around a white inner panel as the core card language.
- Three-column hero mixing portrait, proof and lead magnet.
- Alternating lime and charcoal rhythm down long card stacks.

## Borrow
- Thick coloured frame plus inset white panel to make offers feel like packaged products.
- Offer ladder as a sticky card stack with "what to expect" chips.
- Case study cards that pair a short story with two result figures.

## Avoid
- Very dense; trim to two or three offers for a small business.
- Lime on white text fails contrast; keep lime for fills and frames only.
- Ratings, client counts, revenue figures and press logos are template placeholders; never reuse invented proof.
- The page has a 1px contact anchor and a smooth scroll helper picked up as sections; ignore those crops.
