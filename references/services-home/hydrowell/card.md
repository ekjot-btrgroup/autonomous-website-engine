# Hydrowell  (services-home)
A local trades business site (plumbing, HVAC, electrical) built to convert. It pairs a navy and copper palette on warm cream with an oversized geometric headline, frosted proof cards and lots of reassurance blocks, so it feels friendly, competent and premium for a service trade.

## Type
- H1: Poppins 500, 104px, tracking -2.5px, leading 1.1 (72 / 60). Three stacked words or phrases, the last line in copper.
- H2: Poppins 500, 60px, tracking -2.5px, leading 1.2 (46 / 40); every H2 is two lines with the second line in copper.
- H3: Poppins 600, 26px for card titles.
- Body: Inter 20px for leads, 16 to 18px for UI, 14px for card text; navy at 80% for secondary copy.
- Eyebrow: copper caps text inside a white pill with a copper dot.

## Colour
- Background #f5f2ec cream; Box Background #f0e9e1 for accordion and info cards; white for pricing and project cards.
- Dark Blue #131d2e for headings, icon tiles and the dark feature panel and featured price card.
- Brand copper #ce6837 for the second headline line, primary buttons, check icons and small captions; 14% copper tint for icon halos, 30% for dividers.
- Soft white 72% for glass cards over photos.

## Layout and spacing
- 1120px content with 40px side padding; 80px section padding and a 60px gap from header to content.
- Centred section headers (eyebrow, two-tone H2, one-line lead) alternate with split layouts.
- Cards use 10 to 24px radius; buttons 6 to 10px radius with a white square arrow cap inside.
- Frosted glass cards with background blur sit over photos; light shadows on cards.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed technician photo fading to white on the left, eyebrow pill, three-line headline, lead, copper button and a play-video link; a glass review card and a glass checklist card float over the photo; a four-column trust strip card overlaps the bottom edge.
2. About: heading with copper second line, two paragraphs and button left; a tall photo with a floating glass quote card right; then three stat cards with counters.
3. Services: centred header, 3x2 grid of photo cards with title and short text under each.
4. Why choose: dark photo panel with white headline (middle line copper), copy and button; right column of cream accordion cards with navy icon tiles, the first expanded with text and a photo.
5. Transformations: three before and after style project cards with photo, title, location and date chips.
6. Testimonials: centred header and a horizontally scrolling ticker of review cards with photos.
7. Pricing: centred header, a white bar of three guarantees, then three price cards (middle navy with copper outline) with icon tile, price, check list and full-width button.
8. FAQ: heading and button left, accordion right.
9. Contact: heading, contact detail cards left, form card right.
Footer: cream, logo and blurb, link columns, contact block and fine print.

## Motion
- Headlines reveal word by word: 10px rise, 10px blur, spring stiffness 400 damping 100, 0.5s delay (14 text effects).
- Blocks rise 40px with a spring (stiffness 320, damping 60) after 0.3s (41 appear effects).
- Floating hero cards bob 21px on a 3s linear mirror loop.
- Parallax speed 70 on photos; smooth scroll; fixed navigation.

## Phone
- Hero headline drops to 60px and stacks above the photo; floating cards stack below.
- All grids become single columns; trust strip becomes a vertical list.
- Pricing cards stack with the navy card in the middle.

## Signature moments
- Navy to copper two-tone headlines.
- Frosted cards floating over the hero photo.
- Arrow-capped copper buttons.

## Borrow
- Two-tone headline with the payoff line in the accent colour.
- Overlapping trust strip that bridges hero and next section.
- Dark image panel plus accordion pairing for benefits.
- Guarantee bar above pricing to soften the price reveal.

## Avoid
- Very dense: eleven sections and many cards; cut for small businesses.
- AI-rendered technician photos look staged; use real crew photos.
- Ratings, years and review quotes are placeholders; never ship invented proof.
