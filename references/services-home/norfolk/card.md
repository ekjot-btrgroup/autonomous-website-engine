# Norfolk  (services-home)
A local trades business (built for fencing, fits any outdoor or home-improvement contractor) dressed like a premium architecture studio. The trick is restraint: one geometric sans with tight tracking, a single muted olive accent and dusk photography, so a practical service reads as considered and trustworthy.

## Type
- Onest throughout. H1 70px weight 500, tracking about -3.9px, leading 0.96 (58px tablet, 46px phone): dense, confident, centred.
- H2 46px weight 600, tracking -0.035em, leading 1.08; card titles 24px 600; supporting 20px.
- Body 400 with -0.02em tracking and 1.6 leading in #5f6467; subtitle 17px.
- Eyebrows 14px weight 700, uppercase, +2px tracking, in dark olive: the only tracked-out text, used above every heading.
- Buttons 12px 600 uppercase with 0.045em tracking.

## Colour
- White page dominant, warm off-white (#f6f3ed) for testimonial cards, stone (#d9d4c8) for lines.
- Ink #1e2428 for headings and the one dark band; muted grey for body.
- Olive (#8a8f63, dark #6f7450) only for eyebrows, the nav quote button and the about button: a natural, landscape-derived accent instead of a saturated brand colour.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200px with generous 110 to 130px vertical padding; headers usually split: heading left, short intro right.
- Small 6px radii on cards and buttons, no shadows, hairline card borders.
- Stats block: a 2x2 grid of bordered number cards sandwiched between two tall photos.
- Service cards: image top (about 3:2), title, short copy, small uppercase underlined link.
- Testimonial cards: tall off-white panels, quote at top, name and project at the bottom.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed dusk photo, transparent header with spaced-out wordmark, uppercase links and an olive quote button. Eyebrow, two-line centred headline and subtitle sit in the sky; a small white card with a QR code bottom-right invites a text conversation.
2. Craftsmanship: centred eyebrow and headline, then photo / 2x2 stats / photo in one row.
3. Services: split header, three image cards with uppercase "explore" links.
4. Standard (dark band): eyebrow, headline, paragraph and a ruled accordion of four principles (first open, highlighted in pale olive) left; tall rounded photo right.
5. About: crew photo left, eyebrow, headline, paragraph, checklist and olive button right.
6. Recent work: split header with outline "view all" button, three borderless image tiles with tiny material labels and names.
7. Testimonials: split header and a slow horizontally looping row of tall quote cards.
8. Financing band: dark photo band with headline, line and white outline button left.
9. CTA band: charcoal strip, headline left, short copy and white button right.
10. Footer: centred wordmark, three contact columns, then four link columns and hours.

## Motion
- Hero content fades up 12px, tween 0.62s, ease [0.22,1,0.36,1], 0.08s delay, on mount.
- Sections reveal with the same soft fade-up (14 appear effects total).
- Testimonials loop horizontally: translate -3424px over 58s linear, pausing off screen.
- No hover theatrics recorded; motion is quiet and short.

## Phone
Single column; headline drops to 46px but stays centred; stats become a 2x2 grid with photos stacked; service cards and gallery tiles stack; dark band keeps the accordion above the photo.

## Signature moments
- Headline floating in a soft gradient sky above the product.
- QR "text us" card pinned in the hero corner: a modern, local-business touch.
- The dark standards band with the open accordion item tinted olive.

## Borrow
- Split section headers (title left, two-line intro right) for calm editorial rhythm.
- Tracked uppercase eyebrows in a muted accent above tight-tracked headlines.
- Photo / stat grid / photo trust row.
- Slow linear testimonial loop instead of a carousel with controls.

## Avoid
- Testimonial cards are very tall for short quotes; reduce height if reused.
- Several sections share identical structure (eyebrow, H2, three cards); vary at least one.
