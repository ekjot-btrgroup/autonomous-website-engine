# LEXVARON  (services-legal)
A contemporary law-firm or legal advisory site that trades oak-panel clichés for a clean product-style system. One tight grotesk, a warm off-white canvas, crisp white cards and a single safety-orange accent paired with near-black tiles make it feel direct, modern and trustworthy.

## Type
- Display: General Sans 500, 80px, tracking -0.045em, leading 1.0 (60 / 44). Three short stacked statements.
- H2: General Sans 500, 52px, tracking -0.035em, leading 1.06 (42 / 34); H3 26px, H4 20px.
- Stat: 64px 500, tracking -0.045em, with the plus sign in orange.
- Body Large 19px, Body 16px, Small 14px; muted grey for descriptions.
- Eyebrow: 12px 500 caps, tracking 0.14em, set in a small pill with an orange dot.
- One family throughout; contrast from size and weight 500 against 400.

## Colour
- Canvas #f1f0ec warm off-white; Surface #ffffff for cards.
- Ink #141414 for text, dark cards and the dark button; Muted #6b6a66 for body.
- Accent #ff5a1f orange: primary button, dots, stat plus signs and one highlight tile per row. Accent Soft #ffe7dc for dot halos.
- Lines at 9% ink between feature points.

## Layout and spacing
- 1176px content in 32px padding; sections 110px top and bottom with a 56px gap from header to content.
- Header pattern: eyebrow pill and H2 left, short grey paragraph and white pill button right.
- Cards: white, 22 to 28px radius, generous padding, no shadows; a row of four typically ends black then orange.
- Hero is a rounded 28px photo card inset from the edges with a dark gradient shade.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: photo card with dark shade, eyebrow pill, three-line headline, grey copy, orange and white pills; a white floating card with stacked avatars and a short promise bottom right.
2. About: white text card with heading, paragraph, three ruled feature points with orange dot icons and a dark pill; beside it a tall column photo with a dark overlay card carrying a principle line.
3. Stats: four tiles, black first, two white, orange last, label top and big number bottom.
4. Expertise: header row, 3x2 grid of white practice cards with small icon, title, text and Explore link.
5. Process: header row, four step cards with STEP pills; third card black, fourth orange.
6. Team: header row, four portrait cards with name and role.
7. CTA band: dark rounded panel with heading, copy, orange pill and a photo on the right.
8. Testimonials: centred heading, two quote cards (white and black) with initials avatars.
9. Insights: header row, three article cards with photo, tag pill, read time and title.
Footer: dark panel with tagline, link columns and a giant wordmark.

## Motion
- Content rises 32px and fades in on load with a 1.1s spring, no bounce, 0.1s delay (30 appear effects).
- Sticky navigation. No tickers, parallax or hover choreography: calm and businesslike.

## Phone
- All grids collapse to single full-width cards; stat tiles stack.
- Hero floating card moves below the buttons; display 44px.
- Header rows stack heading over paragraph and button.

## Signature moments
- White, white, black, orange card cadence.
- Orange dots as a consistent micro motif.
- Floating avatar card on the hero photo.

## Borrow
- One saturated accent plus near-black tiles to punctuate a neutral card grid.
- Reusable two-column section header (title left, copy and button right).
- Feature points as ruled rows with tiny coloured dot icons.

## Avoid
- Placeholder stats shown as [XX]; never ship invented numbers.
- Stock legal imagery (columns, gavels, handshakes) reads generic; use the firm's own spaces and people.
- Almost every section uses the same header pattern; vary at least one.
