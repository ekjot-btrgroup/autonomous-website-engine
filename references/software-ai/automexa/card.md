# Automexa  (software-ai)
A site for a studio that designs AI workflow automations for small and mid-size businesses. It sells clarity: a white, orderly page where one warm coral accent marks the important word in every title, and a live-looking workflow card in the hero shows the service instead of describing it.

## Type
- Headings: Geist 600, H1 72/73 with -3.5% tracking (56, 42 smaller), H2 48/52 at -3%, H3 22/27; stat numbers 52 at -4%.
- Body: Inter 400, 18/29 large and 15/24 regular in muted #6d6b73; 13px medium for small UI text.
- Eyebrows: Inter 600 12px uppercase with 14% tracking in coral, preceded by a small coral dot. Tags 11px uppercase 8% tracking.
- Two-tone titles: neutral words in near-black, the key noun phrase in coral (same weight), which becomes the page's rhythm.

## Colour
- White base alternating with warm off-white #f6f5f3 bands to separate sections; borders at 8% ink.
- Ink #0e0e12, muted #6d6b73.
- Coral #ff4d2e is the only accent: eyebrows, title highlight words, step numbers, icon tints, the result step, and an orange-to-pink gradient for featured cards (pricing, tools panel, final CTA).
- Soft coral tint #ffede6 for chips; dark #17171c bands only for a quote banner.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 canvas, 24px side gutters, roughly 110px vertical section padding, sections alternate fill.
- Section heads: either centred (eyebrow, two-tone H2, subtitle) or split (title left, intro + button right).
- Cards: white with 18px radius on off-white bands, light border, small icon square, title, two-line copy. Featured card uses the coral gradient with white text.
- Buttons: pill, black with a coral gradient icon circle at the right, or white with a black circle; both have a soft drop shadow.
- Stats row under the hero uses thin coral vertical rules as separators, big numerals and grey captions.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: eyebrow pill, two-line H1 with coral second line, three-line grey subhead, two pill buttons, a small trust line with a coral icon, then the workflow card in a glowing peach frame; a three-stat row beneath.
2. Tools ticker: grey label and a row of tool names with icons sliding.
3. What we automate: split head, 4-up cards with the first in coral gradient, then a dark quote banner.
4. How it works: centred head, phase tabs, four numbered step cards with big coral numerals and tags.
5. Results: split head, three outcome cards with tags, note line with chips.
6. Case studies: split head with button, three cards with abstract orange renders, tag overlay, title, line and a full-width "read" pill.
7. Industries: centred head, 3x2 grid of plain cards.
8. Integrations: coral gradient panel with copy left and a 2x4 grid of white tool tiles right.
9. Why us: centred head, four principle cards, dark quote banner.
10. Testimonials: centred head, three quote cards with avatar.
11. Pricing: three plans, middle in coral gradient, buttons below.
12. FAQ: left intro with a contact card, accordion right.
13. Final CTA: full-width coral gradient panel with centred headline and white pill; footer with a giant faint wordmark.

## Motion
- 49 appear effects: fade up y 24, spring 0.9s bounce 0.15, on mount; consistent and gentle.
- One ticker at velocity 40 for the tools row, slowing on hover.
- Sticky navigation bar. Otherwise static; the hero card implies motion through its "running" chip and timestamps.

## Phone
- Single column; workflow card steps stack vertically with connectors; stats stack; all grids become one column; pricing stacks with the coral plan first emphasis kept. H1 42px. Nav collapses to logo + menu.

## Signature moments
- The glowing workflow card that shows the service as a running pipeline.
- Coral highlight words in every heading.
- Gradient coral panels for integrations and the final CTA.

## Borrow
- Show the service as a live UI artefact in the hero (steps, statuses, log lines).
- One-word colour emphasis in titles as a cheap but strong system.
- Alternating white and warm off-white bands to pace a long page.
- Pill buttons with an icon disc for a crafted CTA.

## Avoid
- Many sections share the same card grid; vary density when reusing.
- Coral gradient on three large panels starts to feel loud; keep one or two.
- Abstract 3D orange renders are stock-looking; replace with brand-specific imagery.
