# Kairox  (software-ai)
A small AI-automation studio that wants to look like engineers, not marketers. The whole page sits on a visible drafting grid of hairline rails, uses one grotesk at regular weight, and replaces stock photos with isometric line drawings, so it reads calm, exact and expensive.

## Type
- Geist only. H1 64px / 400 / -0.045em / 1.1 (48px tablet, 38px phone); H2 40px / -0.035em (34, 30); H3 26px / -0.03em.
- Body M 16px / 1.5, S 14px / 1.57; XL 22px lead. Medium 500 only for list labels and buttons.
- Tags and labels: 12-16px uppercase, 500, no tracking change, set in small boxed chips.
- A 200px display size exists for the footer wordmark.
- Two-tone headings: first clause in ink, second clause in 40% ink on the same line ("Small team." then grey continuation).

## Colour
- White page, ink #1c1c1e for text and full-bleed dark bands; dark cards #212123 inside dark bands.
- Soft grey #f5f5f6 for alternate panels; hairlines #cfcfd2 on light, #67676b on dark.
- Copper #b1715b and pale steel blue #9fd0de appear only inside the prism visual, never as UI colour.
- Buttons: white pill on dark, black pill on light, always paired with a square arrow tile.

## Layout and spacing
- 1440px canvas with two continuous vertical hairlines 40px from each edge; content lives inside these rails.
- Between sections, a 160px empty band with horizontal rules turns the page into a ruled sheet.
- No radius except pill buttons; no shadows; borders only as hairlines and table rows.
- Asymmetric 40/60 splits: heading and short copy left, data or cards right.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-bleed animated prism streaks, large two-line headline bottom left, short lead under it, pill button plus square arrow tile bottom right.
2. Logos: single row ticker of greyed wordmarks between hairlines.
3. Problem: heading and copy left with a small dark pill; right, an isometric stack of boxes beside a ruled table of time costs and a black total tile with a big figure.
4. Solution: full dark band, heading left, 2x2 dark cards each with an isometric line drawing, title, two lines of copy and two uppercase tag chips.
5. About: warm team photo left, two-tone heading right, two big stat figures and a button pair.
6. Process: four columns numbered with small uppercase tags, H3 step names, short copy, separated by hairlines.
7. Industries: dark band, 3x2 cards with a small icon, name and uppercase chips.
8. Case study: slider of one wide grey card, quote and results left, portrait photo right, square prev and next arrows.
9. Pricing: two plan cards side by side, the second inverted dark with the prism image in its header and a small badge.
10. FAQ: heading and short note left, accordion right with hairline rows and plus icons.
11. CTA: repeat of the prism visual with a big two-line headline and pill button, then a footer with link columns, an email prompt and a huge wordmark.

## Motion
- Appear only: spring 0.7s, bounce 0, delay 0.1s, y 20px, at 20% in view; staggered per card.
- Prism glass flow component animates continuously in hero and CTA.
- Sticky navbar, logo ticker; nothing else moves, which keeps the technical tone.

## Phone
- Rails stay as thin side lines; sections stack to one column with the same 160px rhythm reduced.
- Hero headline 38px; problem table stacks under the illustration.
- Dark bands remain full width; cards go single column.

## Signature moments
- The drafting-grid rails and ruled divider bands that frame every section.
- Copper and steel prism streaks as the only colour on the page.
- Isometric wireframe drawings as product imagery.

## Borrow
- Visible layout rails and empty ruled bands for a precise, engineered feel.
- Ruled data table with a black summary tile to quantify a problem.
- Line-art isometric illustrations instead of stock photos for abstract services.
- Pill button plus separate square arrow tile as a CTA pair.

## Avoid
- The 160px empty bands can feel like dead space on short pages; reduce for fewer sections.
- Three full dark bands plus a dark plan card; balance light and dark when reusing.
- Tiny uppercase tags at 12px can be hard to read in long lists.
