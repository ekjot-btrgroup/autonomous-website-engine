# landio  (software-ai)
An AI automation consultancy shown as a calm, cinematic night scene. Everything sits on a blue-black ground; colour is almost absent, and contrast comes from soft light, frosted dark panels and the switch between a crisp grotesk and an italic serif inside the same headline. It reads premium and quiet rather than loud neon.

## Type
- Headlines: Inter 500, H1 72px with -0.02em tracking (64, 48 on smaller screens), H2 44px (36 on phone), H3 28/120% for card titles.
- The trick: the last two or three words of every H1 and H2 switch to Instrument Serif italic, a little lighter in tone. The serif carries the personality; the grotesk carries the clarity.
- H1 is filled with a soft vertical grey to white gradient so it looks lit from above.
- Body: Inter 14-16, 1.6 leading, around 70% ink; small labels Inter 12-14 uppercase inside pill badges with a tiny glyph.

## Colour
- Ground #04070d (blue-black, not neutral black), card surface #10131c, hairlines at about 7% pale blue.
- Ink is a cool pale grey #d5dbe6 rather than pure white; secondary copy at 70% and a desaturated steel #b8c7d9 at 50%.
- Single accent: a pale ice blue #a6daff, used only as glow (under the hero icon tile, behind buttons, a tiny dot in the hero badge).
- Buttons are near-black with a thin light edge and a faint white glow line along the bottom.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 canvas, 40px sides, 100px vertical section padding; every section opens with the same centered stack: pill badge, two-style H2, one line of supporting copy, then content 40px below.
- Each section has a faint radial "bg shape" light and a top border line, so the page reads as a sequence of softly lit rooms.
- Cards: 10-16px radii, 1px faint border, slightly lighter top edge, no hard shadows. Bento rows mix one narrow and one wide card, then three equal cards.
- Header: fixed bar with logo left, links grouped in a pill capsule in the centre, a dark button with sparkle glyph on the right; a blur gradient strip is fixed at the bottom of the viewport.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: full-height dim smoke video with floating particles; centered app-icon tile with blue under-glow, a dotted uppercase badge, two-line H1 ending in serif italic, one short subline, a dark glowing button, three social glyphs split by hairlines, and a bobbing scroll chevron.
2. Founder note: badge and a large centered statement in muted steel that reveals word by word, with an avatar and role line under it.
3. Benefits: three cards, each with a small abstract chart or diagram illustration above a title and two lines.
4. Services bento: two cards then three; each card holds a miniature animated interface (task chips sliding, app icons orbiting a sparkle core, research panel, code editor, connected nodes).
5. Features: six icon cards in a 3x2 grid, icon in a dark rounded tile, title, two lines.
6. Process: tabbed step strip with a large UI preview panel and checklist copy beside it.
7. Success stories: case card with a portrait photo, quote and two big metric figures, in a slider.
8. Integrations: three ticker rows of app logos scrolling in alternating directions with edge fades.
9. Reviews: grid of quote cards with avatar, name and role, plus a ticker row.
10. Pricing: monthly or yearly toggle, three plan cards, the middle one lifted with a light edge and a badge.
11. FAQ: two columns, a small "still have questions" contact card on the left and an accordion on the right.
12. Comparison: brand mark versus a generic stack icon, two frosted lists with checks on one side and greyed crosses on the other.
13. Team: 3x2 grid of portrait cards with name, role and small social glyphs.
14. CTA: centered headline with serif ending, a button, an email line, then a minimal footer with logo, link row and legal line.

## Motion
- 69 appear effects, mostly on mount and in view: opacity from 0 with scale 0.5 on a long 1.6s custom ease (0.72, 0.21, 0.36, 1.01), which makes elements bloom in slowly.
- Word-by-word text reveal on the founder statement: each word rises 10px from blur 10px with a stiff spring.
- One loop: the scroll chevron drifting 15px on a 2s mirror.
- Tickers on integrations and reviews; particles over the hero video. Nothing scroll-scrubbed.

## Phone
- Hero keeps the centered stack, H1 at 48px over three lines; nav becomes logo plus menu button.
- Bento and grids collapse to one column; pricing cards stack; comparison panels stack with the brand panel first. Section padding drops but the badge, H2, subline opening stays identical.

## Signature moments
- The grotesk to italic serif switch at the end of every headline.
- The glowing icon tile above the hero headline, like an app launching.
- Small live UI scenes inside service cards instead of screenshots.

## Borrow
- One mixed-style headline device repeated with discipline across the whole page.
- A blue-black ground with cool grey ink and a single pale glow accent.
- Slow bloom-in reveals (scale from half size, long ease) for a soft, expensive feel.
- A consistent section opening (badge, H2, subline) to make a long page feel organised.

## Avoid
- Remove the template promo pill fixed near the bottom right.
- The page is long (14 sections) and several sections repeat the same card grid; trim and vary for a real brand.
- Logo tickers and app-icon orbits imply integrations and clients; only use them with real ones from the brief.
- Very low contrast muted copy on near-black; lift secondary text for readability.
