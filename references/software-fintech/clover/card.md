# Clover  (software-fintech)
A business cashback card and spend-analytics product page that wants money to feel light and easy. Pale lime glows, a slanted italic headline and toy-like 3D coins replace the usual navy-and-trust fintech look with something approachable and optimistic.

## Type
- Display: General Sans 500. H1 72px italic, tracking -0.01em, leading 1.2 (54 tablet, 40 phone); H2 44px upright at -0.03em; H3 24px.
- Body: Inter 400/500 at 16-18px, generous 1.8 leading for intros; small italic Inter lines for disclaimers under CTAs.
- Hierarchy is classic SaaS: badge pill, H2, one-paragraph intro, then cards. The italic H1 is the only expressive move.

## Colour
- White and off-white #f7f8f5 bases, each section overlaid with a diagonal gradient from a pale lime-cream corner to white.
- Lime #a9ed42 for badges, chart fills, coin renders and highlight pills; very dark olive #1d1f13 for text.
- Primary buttons are black with a subtle grey-to-black gradient; secondary buttons are pale lime with a thin border.

## Layout and spacing
- 1080 content, 60px padding, 96px vertical rhythm, 80px between header and grids.
- Floating rounded nav bar (white, 16px radius, hairline border) sitting 20px from the top.
- 16px radius on cards and buttons, soft olive-tinted shadows (rgba 29,31,19,0.05), hairline lime borders.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: centred, small lime badge with social proof, two-line italic headline, one-line subcopy, ghost and black buttons side by side, two italic small-print lines, round scroll cue; coin renders float and can be dragged.
2. Why us: three advantage columns with icons over a ticker strip.
3. Benefits: 3x2 grid of white cards (icon, title, two lines).
4. Process: 2x2 cards each with a title and a checklist.
5. Product: three alternating rows of copy plus a white card holding a chart (area, line, spike), each with its own badge and black CTA.
6. Pricing: three plan cards, middle one flagged, feature checklists, trust row beneath and a small "not sure" helper card.
7. Comparison: table of features with check icons versus two competitor logos.
8. FAQ: centred accordion with lime-tinted rows.
9. Reviews: 3-column masonry of star-rated quote cards with avatars, client logo ticker.
10. CTA: centred headline with button, then a large brand-mark footer CTA.

## Motion
- Appear: scale from 0.7 with opacity 0, spring 1.1s bounce 0.2, 1s delay on hero items.
- Text effect on headings: word by word, y 5px, blur 2px, spring stiffness 400 damping 100, 0.05s stagger.
- Coins: loop y 17px mirror over 2s linear plus freeform drag with snap back (inertia 400/30); hover scale 1.1 on small elements; tickers; smooth scroll; fixed blur gradient at the viewport bottom.

## Phone
Single column at about 20px padding; H1 to 40px over two to four lines; product rows stack copy then chart; pricing cards stack; comparison table shrinks to compact columns.

## Signature moments
- Draggable coins bobbing beside the headline.
- Italic hero headline in an otherwise upright system.
- Pale lime diagonal washes giving every band a sunlit corner.

## Borrow
- One expressive italic display line in a neutral SaaS system.
- Product rows that use real-looking chart cards instead of screenshots.
- Comparison table with brand logos as column headers for fintech or SaaS briefs.

## Avoid
- Section-for-section SaaS template structure feels generic; reorder or merge.
- Hand-drawn scribbles add little and read as filler.
- Lime on white for small text or icons fails contrast.
