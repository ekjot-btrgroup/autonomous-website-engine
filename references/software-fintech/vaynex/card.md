# VAYNEX  (software-fintech)
A personal money app pitched as calm, smart and premium. The page opens in a deep forest-green dusk that fades to white, then settles into a rhythm of white sections and pale mint trays. Everything is product UI: a phone, floating frosted widgets and diagrams, no photos. Green reads as money and growth without shouting.

## Type
- Headings: General Sans 600 with tight tracking: display 84 (64, 46), H1 68, H2 52 (42, 34), H3 30, stats 60, statement 150.
- Signature move: the second line or closing phrase of every big heading switches to Instrument Serif italic in the same size, often darker than the grey first half.
- Body and UI: Inter 15 to 18 at 1.55 to 1.6 leading in a muted green-grey; app screens use their own tiny Inter scale (10 to 14).
- Labels: Geist Mono 12 uppercase, 0.08em tracking, inside small white pills with a green dot.

## Colour
- Ink #0b2a22 (green-black), muted #5f706a, lines #e2ebe7.
- Primary deep green #0e5c48 for buttons, icon tiles, one hero stat card and the CTA panel; a softer sage #4fa889 for secondary accents.
- Surfaces: white and mint tints (#eef5f1, #e2f1ea) for trays and cards.
- Hero gradient: #0a3a2f to #11523f to #3e8a70 to #cfe5dc to white, with a faint grid and an aurora glow.
- A coral #ff7a66 exists for rare alerts only.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 canvas, 24px gutters, generous 120 to 150px section padding.
- Alternates two section types: open white sections with a split layout, and mint panels inset 12px from the viewport edge with large radii, so the page feels like a stack of trays.
- Every section opens with a centred mono pill eyebrow, a two-style heading and one or two lines of muted lead.
- Nav: a floating frosted bar with rounded ends; logo tile left, links centre, text login and a deep green pill button right.
- Buttons: fully rounded pills; white on dark, deep green on light, ghost sage as secondary.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: frosted nav, mono eyebrow, two-line headline (bold then serif italic), lead, two pill buttons; centred phone with balance, quick actions and activity, flanked by four frosted widgets (payment toast, savings goal bar, AI note, weekly bars) bobbing gently; thin arc horizon behind.
2. Trusted: a slow ticker of grey partner marks.
3. Stats: mint tray with a giant edge-cropped statement row of three verbs, eyebrow, heading, lead and four slightly rotated stat cards (one in solid green) fanned along the bottom.
4. Overview: split with a phone or dashboard card left and feature rows with icon tiles right.
5. Spend management: mint tray with a large spending chart card and a 2x2 grid of feature cards.
6. Intelligence: centred head, a phone between floating chat-style insight cards and a soft glow.
7. Payments: split with icon feature grid left and a send-money UI right.
8. Investments: centred head, a fan of three overlapping phones over a glowing horizon arc, asset rows.
9. Integrations: mint tray with the orbit diagram and two rows of grey partner marks, closing pill.
10. Security: split with a shield tile on a dark green panel and four security cards.
11. Pricing: mint tray with three plan cards, the featured one in deep green.
12. Final CTA: deep green rounded panel with headline, lead, store badges and floating cards; then a light footer and a giant cropped wordmark.

## Motion
- 145 appear effects: rise 16px on a 0.8s spring with a light 0.12 bounce and 0.1s delay; dense but subtle.
- 11 word-by-word heading reveals (rise 24px from 12px blur, 0.7s spring).
- 26 loops: floating widgets and diagram pills bob 10px on a 3.4s ease-in-out mirror, giving the page a steady breathing feel.
- Three tickers (partner marks) at moderate speed, slowing on hover. No scroll-scrubbed transforms.

## Phone
- Hero stacks with the headline at 46, buttons side by side, phone centred with fewer widgets.
- Mint trays become full-width cards with smaller radii; splits stack copy first; stat cards straighten into a column; pricing stacks with the featured plan first.

## Signature moments
- Forest-to-white gradient hero with a glowing horizon arc.
- Bold grotesk plus serif italic heading pairing used on every section.
- Tilted stat cards under an edge-cropped giant word row.
- Orbit diagram for integrations.

## Borrow
- Alternating open sections and inset rounded trays to pace a long product page.
- Floating frosted widgets around one hero device to show breadth without clutter.
- Mono eyebrow pills as a consistent section opener.
- Slow bobbing loops on decorative UI only, never on text.

## Avoid
- The stat figures, partner marks and app data are placeholders; never present invented totals, users or uptime as real.
- Many sections repeat the "phone plus cards" idea; pick the strongest three for a real brand.
- Green on green inside the hero can lose contrast for small labels; keep white or very light text there.
- The grotesk plus serif italic heading trick is common in this library; vary it when reusing.
