# Ondrel  (software-saas)

A calm, very precise product site for a team planning tool. White paper, hairline rails and rules, one confident grotesk and a cobalt accent; the warmth comes from pastel sky photography behind the product UI. It sells by showing believable screens rather than icons.

## Type
- Inter Display for headings: H1 600 at 72px, -0.035em, 1.0 leading (56 / 42 on smaller breakpoints). H2 500 at 52px, -0.03em; H3 34px; H4 20px.
- Body in Inter 500: Lead 18px / 1.5, Body 15px / 1.55, both in slate grey #6B7382.
- Section headings are one long sentence: the first clause in near-black, the rest continues in grey on the same line.
- Small labels 12 to 13px inside UI mocks; eyebrows are short blue text labels, not caps.

## Colour
- White background, #F4F5F7 surface, #E3E6EB borders, ink #15171C.
- Accent cobalt #2B59FF on eyebrows, progress bars, today markers and links; a red #E5484D only for status.
- Primary button near-black, secondary button white with a border; both 8px radius.
- Pastel pink, lilac and sky blue appear only inside photographic or blurred gradient panels.

## Layout and spacing
- 1200 frame with two 1px vertical rails 32px in from each side running the full page, and a full-width 1px rule between every section.
- Content sits on a wide left column inside the rails; headings left aligned except the hero, quote and closing CTA.
- Image panels have 20px radius; UI inside uses 6 to 8px; status chips and avatars are pills.
- Stats band of four big numbers with small grey captions, separated by the rails.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Sticky nav, then hero: announcement pill, two-line centred H1, two-line lead, two buttons, small reassurance line.
2. Hero showcase: browser-style app window with sidebar and timeline over a sky photo, plus a floating AI update card; logo ticker below.
3. Features: sticky index list on the left, four feature blocks on the right, each a heading sentence plus a real UI fragment.
4. Stats: four large figures in a row.
5. Weekly update: copy and three icon rows left, iridescent panel with a status update card right.
6. Customer stories: three photo cards with short outcome headlines.
7. Pull quote, large and centred, with name and role.
8. Pricing preview: four plan columns with one highlighted.
9. FAQ: heading and button left, accordion right.
10. Changelog preview: dated entries in a timeline list with thumbnails.
11. Closing CTA on a rounded pastel gradient panel, then a footer with a giant faded wordmark.

## Motion
- Hero headline reveals word by word: opacity 0, y 14px, blur 6px, spring 0.7s, 0.05s stagger.
- Showcase and image panels rise in view: from y 56px and scale 0.96 to rest, spring stiffness 500, damping 60.
- Feature index is sticky and swaps variants as each feature block crosses 50% of the viewport (replays on scroll back).
- Logo row ticker velocity 40, slowing to 40% on hover. Small icon tiles scale 1.1 with a tinted background on hover, 0.2s tween.

## Phone
- Rails stay as thin borders; everything single column with 20px sides.
- The app mock is cropped to the timeline and the floating card sits under it; H1 drops to 42px over three lines.
- Feature index disappears; blocks stack with their UI fragments; pricing columns stack.

## Signature moments
- Ruled-paper page structure with rails and rules.
- Product window on a dreamy sky photo: technical content, soft setting.
- Sticky index that tracks the scroll through features.
- Heading sentences that fade from black to grey mid-line.

## Borrow
- Rails and rules as a quiet structure for any precise, trustworthy brand.
- Real interface fragments as feature visuals.
- Photographic pastel backdrops behind hard UI to add warmth.
- Reassurance microline under the hero buttons.

## Avoid
- Section crops in this capture start about 64px high (sticky nav offset), so the hero headline looks cut; judge from the full screenshot.
- Sample logos, user counts and stats are invented placeholders; never reuse figures without facts from the brief.
- The page is long (eleven sections); trim for a smaller product.
