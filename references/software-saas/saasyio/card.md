# Saasyio  (software-saas)
A product site for an AI workflow automation tool aimed at operations and finance teams. It looks like a calm spec sheet: an off-white canvas framed by thin rails, hatched dividers between chapters, and one soft periwinkle used for plates, pills and dot art, so the product UI does the talking.

## Type
- Inter only. H1 48/53 at 500 with -3px tracking (40, 34 smaller); H2 32 at -2.2px; H3 24 at -1.6px; H4 18; card titles 18-20.
- Body 14/18 with -0.8px tracking, which keeps paragraphs compact and techy; article text 17/28 in grey for long reads.
- Hierarchy comes from size and weight 500 against grey body; no display face.
- Section badges: small periwinkle pills with dark text above centred H2s.

## Colour
- Canvas #f8f9f6 off-white, cards #ffffff with #e6e6e6 hairline borders.
- Ink #1c1c1c, grey #787878.
- Periwinkle family: #d1d4fa primary tint for pills and button fills, #979df0 for dot art and accents, #e0e2ff for plates. Deep teal-black #031d22 for the dark results band and featured pricing column.
- Colour appears as a plate behind content rather than as text colour.

## Layout and spacing
- Content lives in a framed 1120 column with 1px rails; 40px diagonal-hatch bands separate every section across the frame.
- Sections use 120px vertical padding with 80px inner side padding; section headers centred with badge, H2 and one-line sub.
- Hero is split: big two-line headline left, short paragraph and button right, then a full-width product window on a dot-field plate.
- Cards: 24px radius, white, thin border, illustration area on top, title and two-line text below. Buttons are dark pills with a periwinkle icon square at the left, or periwinkle pills in the nav.

## Sections (homepage, in order)
1. Hero: badge, two-line headline left, intro and button right, hatched bands above and below, then a browser-style product card (tabs for use cases, prompt sentence, build button, workflow checklist with timings, three KPI cards) on a periwinkle dotted plate.
2. Features: three cards with halftone pictograms.
3. How it works: three step panels with mini UI (tool tiles, prompt card, live status list) and a setup note row.
4. Use cases: grid of team cards with company-style logos and tag pills.
5. Integrations flow: tool nodes on the left and outcome cards on the right connected through a central product hub with curved lines.
6. Solutions: tabbed showcase with a team list and a dashboard panel.
7. Results: dark band with tabs and a before/after timeline bar comparing durations.
8. Customers: large case study with stat, then three proof cards with big percentages.
9. Integrations: six logo tiles in a row.
10. Pricing: three columns, middle one dark with periwinkle highlights, billing toggle.
11. FAQ: narrow centred accordion.
12. Blog: featured post with halftone art and a list of posts.
13. Footer CTA: dark block with large headline, link columns and a dotted skyline strip with a glowing arc.

## Motion
- 40 appear effects: fade up y 24, spring 0.6s bounce 0, on mount; quick and uniform.
- Smooth scroll component; a page transition effect is set. Otherwise motion is implied by UI states (spinners, timers, typing caret).

## Phone
- Rails and hatch bands remain at narrow width; hero stacks headline, text and button above a simplified product card; grids go single column; the integration hub diagram becomes a vertical list. H1 34px.

## Signature moments
- Hatched bands and rails that frame the page like a technical document.
- Halftone dot pictograms in two tones.
- The prompt-to-workflow product card as hero.

## Borrow
- A framed column with section bands for rhythm on a light page.
- Halftone dot illustrations as a cheap, ownable icon language.
- Prompt sentence plus generated checklist to explain an AI product in one glance.
- Tight negative tracking on Inter for a crisp, compact voice.

## Avoid
- Remove the template promo widget floating at the right edge.
- Very small body (14px, tight tracking) hurts readability for older audiences.
- Many sections with similar card grids; trim when reusing.
