Treatments · 01–03

Three families of treatment. One injector.

No lasers, no facials. Each group below is a different tool for a different goal, and Bita will tell you plainly which one fits your goal.

The aim across all three: natural, timeless results that enhance your own features rather than change them.

  1. 01

    Neuromodulators

    Wrinkle relaxers that soften lines made by movement, dosed so your face still moves like yours.

    Products
    Xeomin, Dysport, Daxxify
    Priced
    Per unit
  2. 02

    Dermal fillers

    Placed to restore shape and support where it has softened, always aiming for a result that still looks like you.

    aTemplebUnder-eyecCheekdSmile lines eLipfJawlinegChinPriced per syringe
  3. 03

    Biostimulators and regenerative

    Treatments that ask your skin to build its own collagen over time, including PRF, which is prepared from your own blood.

    Products
    Sculptra, hyperdilute Radiesse, PRF (platelet-rich fibrin)
    Priced
    Per vial, syringe or session

Your first visit · four steps

A plan before a single injection.

New patients always start with a consultation only. Treatment comes after you understand your options and what to expect.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Bita assesses your facial structure and asks what you want to change, and what you want to keep.

  2. 02

    Custom plan

    Your options laid side by side: which treatment, how much, and the price basis for each.

  3. 03

    What to expect

    Bruising, timing and aftercare explained before you decide, so nothing on the day is a surprise.

  4. 04

    Treatment

    Booked when you are ready, by appointment, with the same provider who made your plan.
