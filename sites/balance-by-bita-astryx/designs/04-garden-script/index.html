Your first visit

Four unhurried steps

Every new patient starts with a consultation only. Nothing is injected until you understand your options and what to expect.

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    Tell me what you would like to soften, restore or keep. We start with your goals, not a treatment list.

  2. 2

    A careful look

    I assess your facial structure and how your features move, so any change suits the face you already have.

  3. 3

    Your own plan

    A custom plan with clear pricing, timing and what to expect, including bruising and aftercare.

  4. 4

    Treat and tend

    Treatment by appointment, timed around your calendar, weddings and skincare routine.

Treatments

A short, considered menu

Three families of treatment, chosen and combined in your plan. Results vary from person to person.

  • 01

    Neuromodulators

    Wrinkle relaxers that soften lines from expression while keeping natural movement.

    Used here: Xeomin, Dysport, Daxxify

  • 02

    Dermal fillers

    Gentle, placed volume that restores shape and balance without the overfilled look.

    Areas: temple, under-eye, cheek, smile lines, lip, jawline, chin

  • 03

    Biostimulators and regenerative

    Treatments that work with your own collagen and tissue over time. PRF is made from your own blood.

    Used here: Sculptra, hyperdilute Radiesse, PRF (platelet-rich fibrin)
