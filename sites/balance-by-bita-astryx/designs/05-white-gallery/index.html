The injector

Hi, I’m Bita.

My interest in aesthetics started young, with acne. I’ve been an aesthetics patient myself and worked in several aesthetic roles before I became a physician assistant and trained alongside a renowned injector. Now I run my own practice, and I treat people of every background and ethnicity.

I only offer injectables and regenerative treatments. That focus lets me give every face the attention it deserves, with precision, respect and intention.

The goal is always you, with the cherry on top.

Treatments

Three disciplines. Nothing outside them.

Every plan draws from the same short list, chosen in consultation for your face and your goals.

Neuromodulators

Wrinkle relaxers that soften lines from movement while keeping natural expression.

Products
Xeomin, Dysport, Daxxify

Dermal fillers

Measured support for structure and volume, placed to suit your proportions.

Areas
Temple, under-eye, cheek, smile lines, lip, jawline, chin

Biostimulators & regenerative

Treatments that work with your own skin and build gradually over time.

Options
Sculptra, hyperdilute Radiesse, PRF (platelet-rich fibrin, made from your own blood)

Your first visit

Every new patient starts with a conversation.

No treatment on day one. You leave knowing your options, what to expect and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Bita assesses your facial structure and listens to what you’d like to change, and what you’d like to keep.

  2. 02

    A plan made for you

    You get a custom plan with clear options, timing and costs, so you can decide without pressure.

  3. 03

    Know what to expect

    Before any treatment, we go through bruising, how long results take to show and aftercare.

  4. 04

    Treatment, by appointment

    Booked Monday to Friday, between 10:00 am and 5:00 pm, at a pace that suits you.
