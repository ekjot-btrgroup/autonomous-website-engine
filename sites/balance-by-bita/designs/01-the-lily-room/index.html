02 · The treatment menu

Injectables and regenerative care. Nothing else.

Three families of treatment, chosen and combined for your face at consultation. Results vary from person to person, and Bita will tell you plainly when less is the better plan.

i.Neuromodulators

Wrinkle relaxers, gently dosed

Neuromodulators soften the lines that expression leaves behind while keeping your face your own. Bita uses three distinct products and selects the one that suits your plan.

Products used

  • Xeomin
  • Dysport
  • Daxxify

ii.Dermal fillers

Shape and support, area by area

Filler placed with restraint to restore or refine, never to overfill. Each area is assessed as part of the whole face, so the result reads as rested rather than “done”.

Areas treated

  • Temple
  • Under-eye
  • Cheek
  • Smile lines
  • Lip
  • Jawline
  • Chin

iii.Biostimulators & regenerative

Work with your own skin, over time

Biostimulators encourage your skin’s own support, with results that build gradually. PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood.

Treatments

  • Sculptra
  • Hyperdilute Radiesse
  • PRF

03 · Your first visit

Four steps, at your pace.

  1. I

    Consultation

    Every new patient starts here, with a consultation only. Bita assesses your facial structure and listens to what you would like to see.

  2. II

    Your plan

    A custom plan with your options laid out plainly: what each treatment does, how it is priced and how the timing works.

  3. III

    Treatment

    Your appointment with Bita, on a weekday that suits you. Questions are welcome at every point, including on the day.

  4. IV

    Aftercare

    Clear guidance on bruising, timing and aftercare, so you know what the days after your visit may look like.
