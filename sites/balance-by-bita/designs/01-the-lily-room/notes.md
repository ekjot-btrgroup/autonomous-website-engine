# 01 The Lily Room

Concept: the current plum-and-cream brand, elevated into a luxury hotel welcome. Deep plum room, cream type, dusty-rose details, white lilies.
Fonts: Bodoni Moda (display, italic for warmth) + Jost (body, tracked labels). No motion (brief answer "none"); hover colour changes only.
