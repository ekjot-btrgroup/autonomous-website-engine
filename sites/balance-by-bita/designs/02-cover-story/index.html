The feature

Hi, I’m Bita!

From a teenager with acne to the injector in the chair: how a patient became a provider, and why she still thinks like one.

My interest in aesthetics started early, with acne at a young age. I know what it is to look in the mirror and want to feel more like yourself, and I have been an aesthetics patient myself.

I worked in several aesthetic roles before I became a physician assistant, then trained alongside a renowned injector. Today I run my own practice in Sandy Springs.

I treat people of every background and ethnicity. My aim is natural, timeless results that enhance your own features rather than change them, and a standard of care built on refined skill and plans made for one person at a time.

First visit

Every story starts with a conversation. Not a needle.

  1. Step one

    Consultation only

    Your first appointment is a consultation. Bita assesses your facial structure and listens to what you want, and what you don’t.

  2. Step two

    A plan for you

    She builds a custom plan and walks you through your options, so you can decide without pressure.

  3. Step three

    What to expect

    Before any treatment you will know about bruising, timing and how results typically develop for that treatment.

  4. Step four

    Aftercare

    You leave with clear aftercare guidance and a provider you can come back to for the long term.
