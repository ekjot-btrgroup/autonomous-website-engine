Your first visit

Three calm steps, in this order.

New patients start with a consultation only. Nothing is decided for you, and nothing is rushed.

  1. 1

    A consultation

    Bita looks at your facial structure and asks what you would like to soften, and what you would rather keep.

  2. 2

    A plan made for you

    Your options, explained plainly, including what to expect with bruising, timing and aftercare.

  3. 3

    Treatment, when ready

    You decide if and when to go ahead. The aim is a natural, timeless result that enhances your own features.

About Bita

Hi, I’m Bita!

My interest in skin began early, with acne at a young age. I have been an aesthetics patient myself, so I know how it feels to sit in the chair and hope someone is really listening.

I worked in several aesthetic roles, then trained alongside a renowned injector after becoming a physician assistant. Today I run my own practice, and I treat people of every background and ethnicity.

I work with precision, respect and intention, and with one promise: natural, impactful results that honor your unique beauty. I want you to feel known, never processed.
