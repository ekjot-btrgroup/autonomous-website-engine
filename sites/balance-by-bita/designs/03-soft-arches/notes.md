# 03 Soft Arches: notes

Concept: gentle arch shapes and sage calm, a first visit that settles the nerves of people new to injectables.
Fonts: Gilda Display (headings, numerals, prices) + Karla (body, labels). Palette from direction 3. Motion: none (brief), hover colour changes only.

## Section plan (direction order)
1. Hero: split, text left, tall arched eucalyptus photo right with an offset hairline "ghost" arch; h1 "Come curious. Leave looking like you."; consultation CTA + prices link; three-fact row.
2. Your first visit: surface band, heading column left, three steps with large serif numerals separated by vertical hairlines.
3. Treatments: heading + note split, three arched photo cards (middle one dropped), brand/area pills; rows on phone.
4. About Bita: first-person story from the brief, signature line "You, with the cherry on top.", arched portrait placeholder with line-art botanical.
5. Prices: 2x2 ruled groups by unit, syringe, vial, session; tabular numerals.
6. Questions: grouped accordions (around an event / around other treatments).
7. Kind words: dark band with an arched top edge, one large and two small quote placeholders.
8. Contact: ruled detail list + arched plaster image with map placeholder.
9. Chicago waitlist: arch-topped panel with a 3-field form (mailto).
Footer with contact, results-vary line, legal placeholders. "Aa" larger-text toggle in the nav echoes the current site's low-vision menu.

## Signature moments
- Arch mask on hero, treatment cards, portrait frame, contact image, testimonial band and waitlist panel.
- Hairline three-step first-visit row with large Gilda numerals straight after the hero.
- Price menu as ruled rows grouped by unit, syringe, vial and session.

## Placeholders for the client
- Portrait of Bita; licence type/number/state; medical director; privacy notice.
- All prices and the consultation fee; booking platform URL (CTAs currently go to phone/contact).
- FAQ answers (weddings, bruising, peels, facials, lashes, Accutane).
- Three patient quotes with consent, and the review source link.
- Street address, parking notes, map and studio photo.
