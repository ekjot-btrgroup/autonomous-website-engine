03 Your visit

Four steps, in order.No step skipped.

New to injectables or returning for a long-term provider, you begin in the same place: a conversation, not a needle.

  1. Step 01

    Consultation

    Bita assesses your facial structure and listens to what you want to change, and what you don’t.

  2. Step 02

    A custom plan

    You see your options, the products involved and how they are priced, before anything is decided.

  3. Step 03

    Treatment

    Precise, unhurried work by appointment, planned around your diary, your skincare and any event ahead.

  4. Step 04

    Aftercare

    Clear guidance on bruising, timing and aftercare, so you know exactly what to expect afterwards.
