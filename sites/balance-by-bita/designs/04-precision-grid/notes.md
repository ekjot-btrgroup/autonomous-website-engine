# 04 Precision Grid: notes

Concept: "precision, respect and intention" as a design system. White page, a visible 12-column hairline grid
(4 columns on phone), one weight family (Hanken Grotesk 300/400/500), one calm teal (#2c5e63) for actions only.
No motion (brief animation answer: none); only hover colour changes.

## Section plan (direction order)
1. Hero: asymmetric 7/5 split. H1 "Planned to the unit. / Read as rested, not done." (second clause grey),
   lead naming Bita and her credential, Book a consultation plus View treatments and prices; spec strip of three numbered facts.
2. Treatments (01): comparison table T-01 neuromodulators / T-02 fillers / T-03 biostimulators and regenerative,
   rows Products, Areas, Priced per, What to expect. Stacks into labelled spec sheets on phone.
3. Prices (02): hairline price menu with reference numbers (00.1 to 03.3), priced per unit, syringe, vial or session.
4. Your visit (03): four numbered steps on ink rules.
5. Bita (04): portrait placeholder frame, first-person story from the brief, credential list, three numbered values, room photo.
6. Questions (05): sticky side header plus numbered accordion (weddings, peels/facials, lashes, Accutane, first visit, all skin tones).
7. Patients (06): three numbered review slots with the brief's themes; quotes are placeholders.
8. Chicago (07): teal waitlist band with short form (mailto until a form provider is connected).
9. Contact: large two-tone headline, call and email buttons, phone, email, hours, address.

## Signature moments
- Visible 12-column grid behind the page; every treatment, price line, step, value, question and review is numbered.
- Neuromodulator / filler / regenerative comparison table.
- Two-tone headlines with the second clause in grey.
- Real "A+" larger-text toggle in the header (nod to the current site's low-vision menu).

## Placeholders the client must supply
- Bita's portrait; photo of the actual treatment room.
- All prices and the consultation fee.
- Filler brands stocked; typical onset, duration and session counts.
- FAQ answers (weddings, peels/facials, lashes, Accutane).
- Patient quotes and review source link.
- Street address, licence number/state, medical director, legal name, privacy notice.
- Online booking link (buttons currently call or email) and a waitlist form provider.
