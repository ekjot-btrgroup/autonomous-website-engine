Meet your injector

Hi, I’m Bita!

My interest in aesthetics started early, with acne when I was young. I’ve sat in the patient’s chair myself, so I know how it feels to want help and to want it done gently.

I worked in several aesthetic roles before becoming a physician assistant, then trained alongside a renowned injector. Today I run my own practice, focused only on injectables and regenerative treatments.

I treat people of every background and ethnicity. My goal is simple: you should leave looking like yourself, refreshed and never “done”, and you should always understand your options, from bruising to timing to aftercare.

Your first visit

It all starts with a conversation.

New patients begin with a consultation only. No needles, no pressure, just a clear plan.

  1. Step one

    Consultation

    Bita assesses your facial structure and listens to what you would like to change, and what you would like to keep.

  2. Step two

    Your custom plan

    Together you build a plan around your goals and calendar, with your options laid out plainly.

  3. Step three

    Treatment and aftercare

    You know what to expect before you book: possible bruising, when results tend to show, and how to care for your skin after.
