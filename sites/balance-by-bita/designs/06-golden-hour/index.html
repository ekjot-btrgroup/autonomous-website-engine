Treatments

Three kinds of care, nothing else.

The menu stays short on purpose. Bita chooses from it at your consultation, based on your facial structure and what you hope to see.

NeuromodulatorsWrinkle relaxers

Can soften lines from expression while keeping your natural movement. Results typically appear over about two weeks.

  • Xeomin
  • Dysport
  • Daxxify

Dermal fillersMeasured volume

Restores or refines shape where volume has softened, placed in careful amounts so your features stay your own.

  • Temple
  • Under-eye
  • Cheek
  • Smile lines
  • Lip
  • Jawline
  • Chin

Biostimulators & regenerativeGradual, from within

Encourages your skin’s own renewal, building gradually over weeks to months. PRF is prepared from a small sample of your own blood.

  • Sculptra
  • Hyperdilute Radiesse
  • PRF (platelet-rich fibrin)

Your first visit

What happens, step by step.

You will know your options and what to expect, including bruising, timing and aftercare, before any treatment begins.

  1. Consultation

    Bita studies your facial structure and listens to what you would like to change, and what you would like to keep.

  2. Your own plan

    A personalised plan with the options that suit you, the timing involved and the price, explained plainly.

  3. Treatment

    When you are ready, treatment follows your plan with precision and care, at an appointment that suits your calendar.

  4. Aftercare

    Clear aftercare notes, what bruising to expect and when results typically show, so nothing comes as a surprise.
