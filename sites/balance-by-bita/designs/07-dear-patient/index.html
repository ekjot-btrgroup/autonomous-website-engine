From the desk of Bita

I started on the other side of the chair.

My interest in skin began early, with acne as a young girl. I’ve been an aesthetics patient myself, so I know how it feels to walk in hopeful and a little unsure.

I worked in several aesthetic roles before I became a physician associate, then trained alongside a renowned injector to learn the craft properly. Today I run my own practice, and every appointment is with me.

I treat people of every background and ethnicity. Faces differ, and so do the plans: what suits your structure is the only thing that matters in the room.

P.S.My favourite compliment is when nobody can tell what you had done, only that you look rested. You, with the cherry on top.

Your first visit

We begin with a conversation, not a needle.

New patients start with a consultation only. You’ll know your options and what to expect before anything is booked.

  1. i.

    Consultation

    I assess your facial structure and we talk through your goals, your history and what you’d like to keep exactly as it is.

  2. ii.

    Your written plan

    A custom plan: which treatments, in which order, and the published price for each unit, syringe, vial or session.

  3. iii.

    Treatment

    When you’re ready, by appointment, Monday to Friday. I explain each step as we go so nothing comes as a surprise.

  4. iv.

    Aftercare

    Clear guidance on bruising, timing and aftercare, so you can plan around work, events and your skincare routine.

Planning for a wedding or event? Tell me the date at your consultation and we’ll work backwards from it.

Treatments

Three chapters, and only three.

Injectables and regenerative treatments only. Brand names below are the products I use; your plan may combine them, and results vary from person to person.

No. 1

Neuromodulators

Wrinkle relaxers that soften expression lines by relaxing chosen muscles, dosed so your face still moves like yours.

  • Xeomin
  • Dysport
  • Daxxify
No. 2

Dermal fillers

Placed with restraint to restore or define where you’ve lost volume, never to build a new face.

  • Temple
  • Under-eye
  • Cheek
  • Smile lines
  • Lip
  • Jawline
  • Chin
No. 3

Biostimulators & regenerative

Treatments that encourage your skin’s own renewal, with results that typically build gradually. PRF is prepared from your own blood.

  • Sculptra
  • Hyperdilute Radiesse
  • PRF (platelet-rich fibrin)

Not sure where you fit? That is exactly what the consultation is for.
