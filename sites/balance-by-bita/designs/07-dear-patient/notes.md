# 07 Dear Patient: notes

Concept: the homepage is a personal letter from Bita on ivory stationery, ink-blue Caslon, first person throughout.

## Section plan (direction order)
1. Hero: letter sheet with ruled double margin, letterhead line (what + where), "Dear patient," salutation, h1 "Hi, I'm Bita. You'll still look like you.", short note, sign-off with large Caslon "Bita" signature, CTA. Portrait placeholder clipped with a paperclip; postmark stamp "By appointment only, Mon-Fri 10-5".
2. About: "From the desk of Bita" story (acne, patient herself, aesthetic roles, PA, trained with renowned injector, own practice, all backgrounds), margin notes with the three values, taped botanical print.
3. Process: four roman-numeral steps on a ruled ledger band (consultation only, written plan, treatment, aftercare) + wedding-date note.
4. Services: three ruled menu rows (neuromodulators, fillers with areas, biostimulators/regenerative), brand chips only for brands the brief lists.
5. FAQ: "Notes before your visit" memo pad with ruled margin, six timing notes (weddings, peels, facials, lashes, Accutane, bruising/aftercare), hydrangea photo.
6. Pricing: ledger table per unit/syringe/vial/session.
7. Testimonials: three postcards, quotes placeholders.
8. Waitlist: ink-blue band, wax-seal envelopes, Chicago form (mailto for now).
9. Contact: phone/email/address/hours list + footer with results-vary line.

## Signature moments
- Letter hero with salutation, note and signature.
- Stationery details: ruled margins, postmark stamp, paperclip, tape, postcards.
- FAQ as a memo of timing rules.

Motion: none (brief says none). Only hover colour changes. Text-size toggle nods to the current site's low-vision menu.

## Placeholders the client must supply
Bita's portrait; prices and consultation fee; Radiesse pricing unit; FAQ answers (weddings, peels, facials, lashes, Accutane, bruising/aftercare); three real patient quotes with initials, treatment and source; street address; online booking link; medical director / licence details; privacy notice; waitlist form connection.
