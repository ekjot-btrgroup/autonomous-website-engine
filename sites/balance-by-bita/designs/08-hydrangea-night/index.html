How a first visit unfolds.

No treatment on day one. You meet Bita, talk it through and leave with a plan you understand.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Every new patient starts here. Bita studies your facial structure and listens to what you want to change, and what you don’t.

  2. 02

    Your plan

    A custom plan with the options that suit you, what each one costs and a sensible order to do them in.

  3. 03

    Treatment

    Booked when you’re ready, Monday to Friday by appointment, with time around events and skincare in mind.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    Bruising, timing and what to avoid afterwards are explained before you leave, so nothing comes as a surprise.
