Room 01 · The injector

Hi, I’m Bita.

My interest in skin began with acne at a young age, so I know what it is to sit on the patient’s side of the chair.

I have been an aesthetics patient myself and worked in several aesthetic roles before becoming a physician associate. I then trained alongside a renowned injector, and today I run my own practice. I treat people of every background and ethnicity, and I work with precision, respect and intention.

Room 02 · Index of treatments

Injectables and regenerative work. Nothing else.

A short collection, chosen on purpose. Which of these suits you is decided together at your consultation.

  1. No. 01

    NeuromodulatorsXeomin, Dysport, Daxxify

    Wrinkle relaxers that can soften lines formed by movement, dosed to keep your expression.

    Relax
  2. No. 02

    Dermal fillersTemple, under-eye, cheek, smile lines, lip, jawline, chin

    Small, considered amounts placed to support structure and balance, never to overfill.

    Restore
  3. No. 03

    SculptraBiostimulator

    Prompts your skin’s own collagen; results typically build gradually over a series of visits.

    Rebuild
  4. No. 04

    Hyperdilute RadiesseBiostimulator

    A diluted preparation chosen for skin quality rather than volume.

    Rebuild
  5. No. 05

    PRFPlatelet-rich fibrin

    Regenerative treatment prepared from a small sample of your own blood.

    Regenerate

Results vary by person. Bita will tell you plainly if a treatment is not right for you.
