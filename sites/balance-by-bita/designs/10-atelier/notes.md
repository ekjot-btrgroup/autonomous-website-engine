# 10 Atelier: notes

Concept: a quiet gallery atelier. Taupe walls, Archivo display at weight 400 with tight tracking, Inter body,
one chocolate accent (buttons, dots), white mats around every image, museum-label captions. No motion (brief: none);
hover colour changes only.

## Section plan (direction order)
1. Hero: dot label, stepped two-line h1 ("Every face, / studied like a portrait."), lead + CTA + facts list left,
   gallery wall of three framed plates (marble relief, monochrome tulip, amber bottle) with captions right.
2. About ("Room 01"): portrait placeholder frame, first-person founder story, three values, signature line.
3. Services ("Room 02"): exhibition index, numbered ruled rows (No. 01-05) with brands/areas and captions.
4. Pricing ("Room 03"): dark band, gallery-style price list per unit/syringe/vial/session, placeholder amounts.
5. FAQ ("Room 04"): timing topics (weddings, peels, facials, lashes, Accutane, aftercare) as native accordions + call card.
6. Process ("Room 05"): framed leaf-shadow band, four "rooms" (consultation, plan, treatment, aftercare).
7. Testimonials ("Room 06"): guestbook with three placeholder quotes, one large.
8. Contact ("Room 07"): call/email CTAs + ruled details list.
9. Waitlist ("Room 08"): framed peony + Chicago waitlist form (mailto, name and email only). Footer.

## Signature moments
- Hero gallery wall with three matted frames of different sizes and plate captions.
- Stepped headline (hero and Chicago heading).
- Treatments as an exhibition index.

## Placeholders the client must supply
Bita's portrait; consultation fee and all prices; FAQ answers; patient quotes, initials and review source link;
street address; online booking link; medical director, licence details and privacy notice.
