    Do I need to book?

    Not before noon. Walk-ins are welcome until 12, Tuesday to Saturday. After that, book a chair.

    Three services and four lines on the price list: cut £24, beard trim £12, cut + beard £32, and the hot-towel shave [price to be confirmed].
