Questions

Fair questions, short answers.

Not sure? Just come in.

Before noon there’s no booking needed. Turn up, sit down, wait your turn.

Directions to 12 King Street
Do I need to book?

Not before noon. Walk-ins are welcome until 12. After that, book a chair so you’re not left waiting.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm. We’re closed on Sunday and Monday.

How much is a cut?

A cut is £24. A beard trim is £12, or £32 for both.

Can I get a cut and beard trim together?

Yes. Cut + beard is £32 in one sitting, £4 less than the two on their own.

Do you do hot-towel shaves?

We do: hot towels and a straight razor. [Price to be confirmed by Fade & Co.]
