The shop

A barber you stop thinking about.

Fade & Co does three things: haircuts, beard trims and hot-towel shaves. No lounge, no menu of extras, no lecture on pomade. Just a chair, a steady hand and a mirror at the end.

Tell us what you want, or tell us what you don’t. We’ll take it from there.
