# 02 After Hours: notes (fix round 3, final)

Concept: the shop at night. Near-black, copper only on buttons and the booking choices, Instrument Serif over Manrope, one tungsten grade on every photo.

## Grid
One 12-column grid on the 1200px wrap (24px gutters). Every right-hand block starts on column 7: hero photo, about copy, team photo slot, service index, price list, questions, door sign.

## Section plan (direction 2 order: hero, about, services, gallery, pricing, booking, contact)
1. Hero: two-tone h1 in 3 lines ("The quietest part of your week. Cut, trim or shave."), lead, Book a chair + See prices, then "A day at the shop" ruler (9am to noon walk in / noon to 7pm book; the current part is marked "now" from London time). Chair photo on columns 7-12 with a live open/closed tag.
2. About ("The shop"): h2 + copy; "Who's behind the chair" as a hairline list of chairs (01-03, [Barber name], [Speciality]) beside one captioned 16:9 slot "[Team photo to be supplied by Fade & Co]". No stock people as staff.
3. Services: square photos on columns 1-5; sticky index on column 7 that lights up as each photo passes, with the detail card. Phone: sticky pills.
4. Gallery: steam shave + clippers (6+6), then fade, chair with striped towel, razor line-up (4+4+4). Phone: one wide tile, then a clean 2x2.
5. Prices: "What you'll pay." ledger with dotted leaders; cut + beard £32 with the plain line "Cut and beard together. £4 less than both apart."
6. Booking + Questions: two sections side by side, each with its own h2 (subgrid keeps heads and bodies aligned). The form is two sentences: "I'd like a [haircut] on [Tuesday] in the [afternoon]. I'm [name], text me on [mobile]."
7. Contact ("Visit"): leather band (the only leather use); address set large, Book + directions; "On the door" sign with live status, a 7-day strip (today highlighted), walk-in line and contact placeholders. Footer carries address, hours, walk-ins and phone placeholder.

## Signature moments
- Grey-tail two-tone headlines: hero, services, booking, questions, visit.
- Sticky service index that lights up as the photos scroll past.

## Placeholders for the client
- Barber names, specialities, number of chairs, team photo.
- Hot-towel shave price. Phone number and email. Booking system.
- Photography is Unsplash stand-in.
