# 02 After Hours (Fade & Co)

Concept: the shop at night. Near-black (#0d0d0d), warm copper (#d9824a) only on actions and the active state,
Instrument Serif display with Manrope body. Every headline is one sentence whose second half fades to grey,
a quiet pun on the name. Photos share one low-key tungsten grade (sepia/desaturate/darken, soft copper wash,
inset vignette) so mixed stock reads as one shoot.

## Section plan
1. Nav: serif wordmark ("& Co" greyed), uppercase links, copper "Book a chair" pill; phone menu button with aria-expanded.
2. Hero (big-type editorial): eyebrow with live "open now" status (Europe/London, Tue-Sat 9-7; static hours without JS),
   H1 "Cuts, beards and hot-towel shaves. / On King Street, without the fuss.", lede, primary + secondary CTA,
   tall 4:5 chair photo, three ruled fact cells (walk-ins before noon, hours, cut from £24).
3. The shop (about): two-tone statement, short paragraph in the brand voice, wide tools photo.
4. Services: sticky numbered index on the left (01 Haircut, 02 Beard trim, 03 Hot-towel shave) that lights up copper
   as each photo and description scrolls past; clicking an item scrolls to it. Phone: stacked blocks, no sticky index.
5. Gallery: asymmetric grid (wide + portrait, then two portraits and a quote tile); hover "turns the lamp up".
6. Night band: full-bleed night photo, scrim, two-tone statement on hours and walk-ins.
7. Prices: centred ruled list with dotted leaders, serif tabular prices, "cut + beard saves you £4" (24 + 12 - 32).
8. Booking: two-tone heading, "Good to know" accordions (facts only), request form card (service, day Tue-Sat, time).
9. Visit/footer: giant two-tone wordmark, address with directions link, hours, contact, legal line, photo credit.

## Signature moments
- Headlines whose second half fades from ivory to grey as they enter the viewport (instant grey with reduced motion or no JS).
- Sticky service index that lights up as the photos scroll past.

## Placeholders for the client
- Phone number and email (not in brief).
- Hot-towel shave price (not in brief).
- A line from a regular for the quote tile.
- Booking form is front-end only: connect to their booking system.
- Photos are Unsplash stand-ins (credits in img/credits.json); swap for shots of the real shop when available.
