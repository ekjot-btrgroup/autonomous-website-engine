# 04 Barber Pole Poster: Fade & Co

Concept: the shop's front window as a screen-printed street poster. Deep red paper, cream ink, heavy Anton
headlines, yellow price stickers slapped on top. Photos are treated one way only: black-and-white, high contrast,
either printed straight onto the red (multiply, two-colour screenprint) or hand-cut and stuck on with a white border.

## Section plan
1. Nav: wordmark with a small barber-pole mark, five anchors, yellow "Book a chair". Phone: menu button + full panel.
2. Hero (collage): poster masthead strip (address / hours / walk-ins), h1 "A proper cut on King Street.", one line
   on what we do, Book a chair + See prices. Right: big two-colour printed profile, hand-cut beard-trim photo, three
   yellow price stickers (Cut £24, Beard £12, Cut + beard £32) and a cream "walk-ins before noon" tag.
3. Ticker 1 (yellow, tilted): services, hours and walk-in line on a loop.
4. Pricing (cream paper, red ink): "What it costs." Price board with dotted leaders, huge tabular prices, a "Saves £4"
   sticker on the combo (24 + 12 - 32, arithmetic from the brief), shave price as a marked placeholder.
5. Services (red): "Cut. Trim. Shave." One large feature (the cut) plus two stacked (beard, hot-towel shave), each a
   hand-cut photo with a short dry description.
6. Ticker 2 (cream on deep red, opposite direction).
7. Team (deep red): "Who's holding the clippers." Three mugshot cards on a height-chart backdrop, SVG silhouettes,
   name and speciality placeholders.
8. Gallery (red): "Fresh off the chair." Six pinned black-and-white prints with tape, slight rotations.
9. Booking (yellow): "Book a chair." Request form (name, phone, service, day Tue-Sat, time of day) beside
   "Before you come in" Q&A answered only from brief facts (covers the brief's FAQ).
10. Visit (red): "12 King Street, Leeds." hours table, phone/email placeholders, directions link, animated pole.
11. Footer (deep red): full-width SVG wordmark, small links, legal line.

## Signature moments
- Hero collage: printed profile + hand-cut photo + yellow starburst price stickers that slap on at load.
- Tilted service tickers between sections (yellow on red, cream on oxblood, running opposite ways).
- Supporting: barber-pole stripes (nav mark, visit section), height-chart mugshots in the team section.

## Placeholders the client must supply
- Phone number, email address, payment methods, company/legal line.
- Hot-towel shave price.
- Barber names, specialities and portraits (team section uses silhouettes).
- Gallery and service photos are Unsplash stand-ins: swap for the shop's own work when available.
- Booking form is front-end only: connect to the shop's booking system or inbox.

## Type and colour
Anton (display, uppercase) + Archivo (body; expanded width for labels). Six sizes: hero, h2, h3, lead, body, label.
Red #8f1d17 dominant, cream #fff8ee ink, yellow #f4c430 only on stickers, the primary button, ticker and booking.
Deep oxblood #2b0a08 for the team/footer blocks and as ink on yellow.

## Build notes
- Hero h1 runs up to 132px (about 7.8x body) on purpose: it is the poster headline. Section h2s stay within 5x.
- Services: a razor still life is printed straight onto the red (same multiply treatment as the hero) to balance the column.
- Self-critique (desktop + phone): no item below 4; weakest are Imagery (stand-in stock until the shop's own work)
  and Team (placeholders by necessity). Review: ok, slop: clean.
